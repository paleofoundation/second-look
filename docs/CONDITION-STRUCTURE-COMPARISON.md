## What differs

| Area | Verified live behavior | Adopted planned behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article/body and contents | Overview, Options, Mechanism of Action, Safety, Comparisons, followed by Evidence, Research Feed, FAQs, Update History, References. | Overview, Interventions, Safety, then the same five-part reader tail. Body, contents and citation traversal must share one typed section plan. |
| Intervention explanation | Options and a condition-wide Mechanism of Action remain forced modules. Parkinson's Options still center on the metformin pilot. | Interventions explains the condition's investigated drugs and exact purposes, with intervention-specific rationale and useful comparisons. A condition has no single pharmacological mechanism. |
| Mapping missing labels | `sectionDestination` sends an unrecognized condition label to Options. Merely authoring an Interventions label would therefore not implement the plan. | Explicit mappings and honest missing/unmapped content; no silent fallback into Options or empty optional links. |
| Mechanism data | An authored condition mechanism record suppresses the additional context list. Exact drug/condition mechanisms already exist separately for outcome drawers. | Reconcile all relevant intervention contexts without transferring a condition overview wholesale to one drug. Keep proposed, tested and inferred steps distinct. |
| Evidence table | Already five columns: Intervention, Outcome, Grade, Effect, Evidence. Outcome groups open the existing right research panel. | Retain those five meanings and the separate A-F benefit assessment. The older four-column certainty proposal in CONDITION-PAGES.md was superseded October12 and must not be restored. |
| Scope and completeness | Available selected sources and existing outcome joins can leave a condition parent narrow even when reverse links work. | A condition-led, reproducible search and candidate disposition map beyond the initial drug seeds, with pending, null, harmful and preclinical findings retained. Neither page depth nor a populated table establishes completeness. |
| Safety and right rail | Existing condition safety, snapshot and related reading use the current selected records. | Attribute restrictions to each investigated drug/regimen; show truthful condition/search scope. A three-link related-reading rail is not the intervention inventory. |
| Stable links | Options, mechanisms, comparisons and source/question fragments are current destinations. | Preserve aliases for `#section-options`, `#mechanisms`, `#section-comparisons` and legacy source/question fragments at meaningful new destinations. Preserve citation numbering, drawers, keyboard behavior and reader access. |

## What a future template implementation needs

A separately scoped implementation must update the existing shared composition and types, article rendering, condition/intervention mechanism mapping, source/citation traversal, data projection and authoring paths together. It must migrate supported content into the correct sections, preserve the five-column table and exact outcome records, and supply meaningful aliases rather than discard old anchors. Do not create a competing renderer or treat a navigation rename as complete migration.

Scientific coverage is a separate prerequisite. Reconcile condition candidates, actual sources/access, exact drug-condition-purpose/population/regimen/outcome scope, contradictory results, assessment fingerprints and pending Q01-Q15 work. The four-page depth draft is a selected-source improvement, not the condition-wide search/disposition ledger needed to claim a complete roundup. Vitiligo and the 11 CP027/CP028 pages remain on the explicit08:12 hold; there is no inferred exception for a new template.

Acceptance must include varied condition pages, a multi-drug cancer page, prevention versus treatment, sparse/preclinical findings, a combination, overlapping reports and pending evidence. Verify drugs retain their own composition, every accepted row appears once, content/navigation/citations share order, legacy fragments reveal the right content, filters/reset/back work, and outcome/grade/evidence controls preserve the right drawer and focus behavior.
