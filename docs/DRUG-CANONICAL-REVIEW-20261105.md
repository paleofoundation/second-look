## Official-label additions

Ivermectin uses its already cited oral Stromectol label. Melphalan adds the [official HEPZATO KIT label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=4f83c8f7-4cc0-4219-88d5-7cfddce91198), specifically for its liver-directed product. Methylene blue adds the [official intravenous PROVAYBLUE label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=4f6848e5-35ed-4046-b13c-3032b5ba3232). Neither label is generalized across all routes or research uses. No outside efficacy papers were added.

Methylene blue and melphalan still lack supported grouped outcome matrices in the existing records. Their honest empty outcome states remain; no matrix, benefit grade or trial count was copied from Metformin. Niclosamide’s correction is a correction to a trial, not another trial. Ivermectin’s retracted liver-cancer report remains excluded.
