## Offers and sequence

- Individual topic guide: **$49 USD once**, protected web guide, reader-requested PDF, permanent topic ownership including future approved editions, and retained edition history.

Pending products say “Explore the [Topic] Guide,” released products “Get…,” and verified entitled readers “Open your guide.” Unavailable products cannot create Checkout sessions.

Use hosted Stripe Checkout and its customer portal with server-authoritative prices and signed-in account identity. Verify webhook signatures and successful payment before fulfillment; support pending/failed/delayed payments, duplicates/out-of-order delivery, refund/dispute state, renewals, cancellation and expiry. Exact edition approvals bind web manuscript, PDF, summary and marketing hashes. Clinical and legal approval identity, scope, qualifications, conflicts, decision and dates remain mandatory.
