# Parent topic scope and discoverable directories

The owner's 9 October 2026 Metformin failure report clarifies an existing requirement: a parent entity guide must represent that entity, and the site's main content families must be obvious to navigate. This is a reusable product contract, not permission to invent missing evidence or expand an explicitly narrow project.

## Parent entities and scoped questions

Define the page's editorial scope before choosing its available studies. Entity, condition, outcome and question are separate content types with stable identities and reciprocal links. `/drugs/metformin` is the drug parent; a prevention or treatment question about one condition is a scoped child. Do not let the earliest ingestion packet, most available papers, or original pilot question silently determine a parent guide's scope.

A parent drug page provides an orienting, source-supported description, original/established-use context with its actual verification boundaries, an overview of investigated conditions, and meaningful condition/outcome synthesis within the declared assessed coverage. Its Benefits, Mechanisms, Safety, Results, FAQs, context modules and links must also honor that parent scope. Changing the title or overview and appending a few table rows does not repair a single-condition body. A condition-specific safety finding does not establish drug-wide safety; keep its indication, population and limitations attached. Reuse the separately maintained scoped synthesis records for deeper child answers.

Maintain a versioned page-scope/coverage map linking the parent, relevant condition/outcome questions, demand or editorial rationale, available sources, investigation state and destinations. Separate established context, investigated repurposing, inconclusive/negative results, and topics awaiting assessment. The map records questions and evidence coverage; it must not look like an efficacy ranking. Include meaningful contrary/null findings and keep sparse areas explicit. Owner preferences and internal planning remain private under the reader-copy contract.

Plan necessary missing context and breadth inputs in the existing pre-request ingestion plan before any new acquisition or extraction. A narrow corpus does not authorize assertions from memory, invented mechanisms, clinical grades or a false comprehensive guide. Use available eligible context and completed scoped syntheses, then report the exact remaining content work. Preserve closed-packet/no-web limits. Unassessed topics can be displayed honestly, but broad placeholders alone cannot pass finished-parent acceptance. The 30-drug/30-condition portfolio remains specific to drug repurposing; no universal number of uses per entity is imposed.

Question pages preserve their exact prevention/treatment, population and endpoint scope, with a clear parent link and breadcrumb. The drug name in global discovery links to the parent; a scoped child link must name its question/condition. Avoid duplicate competing parent renderers. Re-ingest, topic projections and content regeneration must preserve this ownership and scope map rather than restoring the original narrow pilot prose.

## Main directory navigation

Each primary content family has a plainly named directory entry in main desktop navigation and in the first level of mobile navigation. For drug repurposing these are **Drugs → /drugs** and **Conditions → /conditions**. Other topics configure their appropriate reader-facing taxonomy, such as Peptides and Stacks, without inheriting drug terminology.

Both complete directories are reachable by a clear click/tap from any public content page without search, hover, footer discovery or knowledge of a URL. A megamenu may show interest-ranked featured entries and a Browse all link, but an ambiguous Explore trigger or featured set alone does not satisfy this requirement. Preserve the uncluttered source-theme styling, keyboard/touch access and direct routes; don't restore decorative dropdown clutter to make the labels visible.

Directory pages enumerate the actual canonical entity or condition registry with readable names and links, useful browse/search/filter behavior, and honest distinctions between published guides, unfinished profiles and planned targets. Counts must reconcile to that registry; 30 drugs means 30 distinct drugs, not 30 studies, answer pages or drug-condition pairs. Do not claim every directory entry is a completed evidence guide. A condition entry opens that condition's overview of relevant drugs/outcomes; it must not be a mislabeled paper catalogue. Both directions link back to their directory and to the corresponding scoped evidence.

Keep featured popularity decisions separate from full taxonomy coverage. Rebuild menus/directories from shared validated configuration and content identities. Do not repair only the homepage, bury the links again in a regenerated mega configuration, or maintain a second inconsistent directory list.

## Acceptance before scaling or declaring the pilot successful

Verify these tasks in actual desktop and mobile rendering, including generated routes:

1. From the homepage and an article, find Drugs and Conditions by their visible labels, open both full directories and reach a parent drug and condition. Repeat with keyboard and touch. Check header and mobile navigation, not just footer links or route existence.
2. Read the Metformin-equivalent parent without opening studies. Explain what the drug is, how original context differs from investigated uses, which condition/outcome areas are actually covered and where uncertainty remains. Inspect all major prose/FAQ/context sections for leftover single-condition framing; more table rows alone cannot pass.
3. Open a condition-specific question, return to the drug parent, then traverse to a condition overview and its relevant drugs. Distinguish each page's scope from titles, breadcrumbs and content without guessing.
4. Trace every displayed condition/outcome conclusion to its actual records. Exclude invented broad conclusions and inappropriate transfer of a condition-specific result or safety finding to the entire drug. Record missing acquisition, synthesis and review separately from layout completion.
5. Repeat content generation or import against unchanged inputs and exercise a second synthetic topic through the same theme/configuration. Directory labels/routes, parent ownership, topic breadth, hero actions, citations, author profile and access rules survive unchanged. Keep synthetic data out of the real site.

Required failure examples: a Metformin heading over a Long COVID article; a general overview over condition-specific Benefits/Safety/FAQs; Drugs/Conditions only in a footer or generic Explore menu; directory entries that all lead to paper lists; inflated coverage from answer-page counts; invented clinical breadth; and generated routes reverting to an old narrow renderer. Record these as product failures even if build, route, schema or component tests pass.

For the existing pilot, repair the shared parent-guide composition and navigation/data adapters as one coherent change. Retain validated corpus IDs, source files/receipts, account records and functioning modules. A structural rebuild does not require deleting the project, redoing completed ingestion or erasing the frozen rule baseline. Record the actual repaired theme/version and independent rendered acceptance; do not call it complete while required content remains unfinished.
