# Consumer guides: a reusable product, not a pricing placeholder

Contract added with One Match Rule v1.19, 9 October 2026. Read alongside master sections 3–5, 8–11 and 13. Applies to the guide product workstream and its shared theme components; it does not activate sales or override a site's existing release/access policy.

## Discover products and choose concrete prices

Generate a guide opportunity registry from the actual question clusters, owner priorities and evidence map. For each candidate record its audience, reader decision, scoped topic/conditions, demand-source IDs or explicit editorial rationale, competing/free substitutes, unique paid value, evidence gaps, review/update effort and release readiness. Rank demand separately from clinical merit and commercial feasibility. A popular disease is not proof a useful, safe guide can yet be sold. Preserve important owner-requested candidates when research is incomplete, with a stated next step in private planning. Do not expose owner preferences/requests or internal selection and batch fields on catalogue/product/PDF surfaces; use neutral topic descriptions and useful readiness explanations.

Choose named initial products and an explicit amount, currency and billing basis for each proposed offer. Record rationale, maintenance/support/review costs, scenario assumptions and the proposed test. An untested price is a price hypothesis, not validated willingness to pay; an owner-adopted price and an active server price are separate states. Do not leave every price as TBD, copy another site's old price as universal, or claim market benchmarking without inspecting current sources. Missing merchant details hold activation, not reversible price planning.

Evaluate a useful free reading resource, focused entity/condition guides and coherent bundles. When subscription guides are requested, define which guide IDs/editions are included, access while active, allowed downloads, update cadence, cancellation/expiry treatment and any retained rights. Specify separately purchased immutable editions independently of membership. Do not promise unlimited future guides or perpetual updates without an adopted, supportable policy. For Second Look, subscription inclusion is explicitly requested (9 October 2026); this does not end the existing free-authenticated research-review phase.

## One product and edition model drives every surface

Use versioned canonical product/edition/offer records, with at least:

| Record | Fields needed by the product |
|---|---|
| Guide | Stable product ID, title/slug, audience and decision, scope, demand/question IDs, truthful promise, contents, exclusions, author assignments, topic links |
| Edition | Edition ID/version, evidence cutoff, updated date, source/claim/review ledger, manuscript/asset provenance, full and summary file hashes, preview route/file, actual editorial/clinical approval record, release status |
| Offer | Product/edition or bundle IDs, amount/currency, one-time or recurring interval, price status, subscription inclusion, update/retention rights, merchant/tax/refund/support dependencies, commerce availability |
| Entitlement | Authenticated user, authoritative order/membership source, product AND eligible edition, active/pending/refunded/expired state, effective dates and download rights; no browser-granted ownership |

Generate catalogue cards, product details, pricing, previews, My guides and secure delivery from these same records. A business-plan document alone is insufficient. The signed-out catalogue has useful nonempty cards: what the reader receives, intended audience, contents/preview, edition/review state, planned or active price, subscription inclusion where adopted, and an honest action. Mark unreleased offers “Planned price” and “In development” (or equally clear wording); no Buy button or member download for an unapproved edition. Do not turn an outline into a promised complete product.

Provide a clearly named Guides destination in primary desktop/mobile navigation, a homepage section/path and the footer. Do not bury it under Learn/Research or confuse it with ordinary free evidence articles. Public previews contain only their explicitly released preview content; paid files remain private. A public preview must never be the full protected manuscript under a different URL.

My guides is a real signed-in account destination, separate from Saved research. Show separately purchased and subscription-included resources with accurate access source, eligible editions, updates, order/status and download actions. Provide useful empty, pending, expired, refunded, retry and missing-file states. Expired research or guide membership cannot erase independent purchase rights. Preserve valid login return destinations. Implement and test the library before sales begin, using isolated test entitlements where appropriate; fixture success is not a live payment or verified customer purchase.

## Shared guide manuscript and PDF renderer

The [packaged guide renderer](theme/releases/README.md) is an available implementation starting point. Use its editable schema/configuration and synthetic proof, respect its recorded limitations, and verify actual destination artifacts rather than rewriting the renderer or treating the included draft as approved content.

Extend the versioned site theme with reusable manuscript/content schema, cover, front matter, chapter/entry, table, callout, figure, FAQ, worksheet, bibliography and contributor components. Consume site brand tokens and canonical accepted evidence through documented adapters. Store editable manuscript/configuration, asset provenance, source mappings and rendered hashes. A new topic changes content/configuration rather than duplicating layout code. Do not package the user's supplied commercial PDF as a redistributable theme asset. Use canonical assigned contributor records for full, preview and summary credits. Whenever an ORCID is displayed in a PDF, render its small green iD logo, visible identifier and correct individual hyperlink through one reusable PDF component; do not leave a second plain-text ORCID credit embedded in manuscript prose. Apply master §7’s identity safeguards, while adapting its web hover/hero behavior appropriately to static PDF credits. Regenerate and inspect all affected variants after author-component changes, preserving prior edition files and recorded hashes.

Use this original structure, adjusted to topic and scope:

1. Cover: clear guide title, useful original art, brand, edition/date and actual writer/editor/reviewer assignments. Never show a pending reviewer as having approved the edition.
2. Linked contents/bookmarks, audience/scope/exclusions, evidence cutoff and how to read findings, effect estimates, certainty and limitations.
3. Concise orientation and decision overview, including visible comparison/findings tables and the important limits of the whole guide.
4. Consistent substantive entries: what the option/question is, source-supported original/repurposing mechanisms and their limits where relevant, who/what was studied, findings and magnitude, certainty/directness, decisive limitations, harms/interactions, regulatory context where relevant and linked source/review references.
5. FAQs mapped to actual demand and editorial questions, with supported answers; questions to take to a qualified professional and usable research-reading worksheets where appropriate.
6. Printable summary retaining the same decisive harms and uncertainty; numbered sources, correction/update route, contributors/conflicts and edition details.

Do not copy the reference's medical judgments, recommendation categories, doses or instructions to assemble drug combinations. For a drug-repurposing guide, descriptive study exposures do not become a self-treatment schedule. Population, subtype/stage, route, comparator and endpoint remain attached to each finding; cancer types and clinical/surrogate outcomes must not be silently combined. No unsupported “best drugs” table or invented safety ranking.

Generate an actual complete first guide from accepted material, plus its preview and summary, under the master's product gate. If the clinical corpus is insufficient, produce an accurately scoped evidence-reading guide where supported and retain condition-specific manuscripts as identified drafts with input gaps. Preparing a draft is useful work, but does not finish the requested clinical guide or permit selling it. Keep guide input requirements and missing primary/safety sources in the ingestion plan before the next affected fetch or ingest.

Use selectable readable text, clear hierarchy, purposeful whitespace, working citations and internal navigation. Reflow tables with units and repeated headers; keep small tables together where practical and group figure titles/images/captions. Avoid orphaned headings, clipped content and unreadable source text. Use the site's palette, including pale-accent treatment, rather than copying Examine's identity. Validate paper geometry for the product; neither 91 pages nor US Letter is a universal requirement. Keep semantic reading order, contrast and meaningful image descriptions, and report any PDF accessibility limitations honestly.

Render and inspect every page of generated release candidates, test TOC/bookmarks/page numbers/citations/links and summary consistency, and preserve the editable source with the release. Demonstrate a second clearly synthetic topic using the SAME renderer with configuration/data alone, without putting fixture claims into production. This is part of G4T; a PDF screenshot or prose specification is not an implemented guide theme.

## Medical communication and release

Provide an original, topic-appropriate medical notice, a fuller linked policy, and a recorded jurisdiction/clinical review. A disclaimer cannot repair unsupported claims or a misleading overall impression. Review titles, cover art, tables, summaries, previews, sales copy and subscription promises together, not just the policy page. Do not copy the 2020 reference's liability language or promise immunity from liability.

For a serious-condition guide such as breast cancer, prominently state the educational purpose and limits near the product promise and at the start of the guide. Readers must not have to buy, log in or expand a footer to see the essential notice. Repeat only material context-specific cautions beside relevant claims; avoid boilerplate in every table cell. Provide a concise notice on catalogue/product/preview surfaces and before any later checkout; include the full notice in the PDF and its summary. Do not present this notice as a required legal waiver or a substitute for consent/terms.

Draft wording to adapt and obtain appropriate review for—not a legal certification:

> This guide explains research for education. It is not a diagnosis, prescription or personal treatment plan, and it does not establish a clinician–patient relationship. Do not start, stop or change medicines, or delay or replace cancer care, because of this guide. Discuss potential uses and interactions with your treating oncology team and pharmacist. A drug approved for one use may not be approved or adequately supported for another. Findings may not apply to your cancer subtype, stage, other treatment or health circumstances. Evidence is current only through the stated cutoff, and important uncertainties may remain. For urgent medical concerns, contact appropriate local medical services.

Adapt that wording for non-cancer subjects rather than copying an oncology warning everywhere. Verify use-specific regulatory status by jurisdiction and date; off-label status alone is neither proof of benefit nor proof that a use is inappropriate. Where practical context is included, preserve the distinction between research description and individualized care.

Before commercial release of a health guide, record an independent appropriately qualified clinical review of its exact edition and marketing claims; an oncology guide requires relevant oncology expertise and medication/interactions review. Resolve material source/correction, safety and applicability gaps. Obtain qualified legal review for applicable jurisdiction, consumer/advertising claims, disclaimer and sale/subscription terms before activating that health-guide offer. Record identity, scope, conflicts, decision, date and reviewed hashes. These are project publication gates responding to the owner's medical/liability concern, not a claim that these instructions determine all legal obligations. Author identity, ORCID, agent checks or successful PDF generation cannot stand in for those reviews. Complete all reviewable drafts and UI work while external review remains HOLD.

Primary background checked 9 October 2026: [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) explains substantiation and misleading net impressions; [FDA off-label explanation](https://www.fda.gov/patients/learn-about-expanded-access-and-other-treatment-options/understanding-unapproved-use-approved-drugs-label) distinguishes approved drugs from unapproved uses; [NCI complementary and alternative medicine](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/cam) stresses discussing nonstandard approaches with the cancer-care team. These US sources inform the safeguards; they do not establish the merchant's governing jurisdiction or approve a guide.

## Reference observations and acceptance

The supplied **Healthy Aging Supplement Guide**, cover edition September 2020, has 91 pages; SHA-256 `c71dccaa1e0f5b7ac70e25060c77ef53fdda3b4d45b3408c1c5fbe303d4d3848`. Page locators are one-based PDF pages. Observed patterns: cover/credits p1; linked contents p2; disclaimer p3; reading key p4; recurring entry/table/warning format pp20–21 and 39–40; FAQ starting p66; sources starting p77; contributor bios pp90–91. Text was extracted for all pages; fourteen representative pages were visually inspected. The source is a design benchmark, not current medical evidence or a prescription template. The printable summary and two-topic reuse proof above are new implementation requirements, not features falsely attributed to the supplied PDF.

G4/G6/G7 acceptance must demonstrate: signed-out homepage → Guides → product/preview; visible actual proposed prices and clearly separated availability; login → My guides → correctly entitled edition; subscription inclusion and expiry semantics; cross-account/anonymous denial of private files; pending/refund/download failure behavior; no sale of an unreviewed draft; and complete rendered guide/preview/summary with matching records. Use safe fixtures/test environments for commerce checks. Record separately product-content, theme, account, clinical/legal review and commerce status. Missing merchant or research inputs cannot excuse an absent catalogue/library, and a completed interface cannot erase missing medical work.
