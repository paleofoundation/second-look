# Mechanisms and reader-facing information

## Source inputs before acquisition or ingest

Persist the mechanism schema and input map in the versioned ingestion plan before its dependent request/import. Every drug has an original-use mechanism record and distinct condition-specific repurposing records. Extract canonical entity/condition IDs, target/pathway/action, source-supported causal steps and measured step, condition relevance, model/species/tissue, formulation/route/exposure/concentration/duration where reported, directly tested versus source-proposed or inferred basis, uncertainty/disagreement/alternatives and translation limits. Each material claim, diagram arrow and number has a source ID, tier, exact locator and extraction status. Missing data is explicit; do not create a mechanism from memory or outcomes alone. Preserve PRIMARY versus review/textbook CONTEXT and mechanistic versus clinical evidence.

## Shared article module

The left navigation label is **Mechanisms**; its stable link reaches **Mechanism of Action**. Every drug page inherits it; applicable intervention, condition and guide contracts use the same typed renderer. Explain original action and the rationale for each investigated condition in plain language, then link to source details and relevant clinical outcome rows. Distinguish demonstrated steps from hypotheses and inconclusive/conflicting findings. A pathway diagram or laboratory finding is not proof of treatment efficacy, and source experimental exposures are not prescribing advice. No heading-only module or invented “why it works” text passes. Maintain usable mobile navigation and no duplicate sections after reruns.

## Private provenance and useful reader copy

Keep owner-requested priorities, personal preferences, selection flags and internal worker/task/batch/ingest notes in private governance. Use explicit allowlisted reader-safe projections for page/configuration props, hydration, APIs/search, product records and downloaded guides. Merely hiding an owner label in CSS does not protect a still-delivered preference field. Render neutral topic groups and titles without disclosing who requested a candidate; do not replace missing demand evidence with a “most popular” label.

Retain substantive source limitations, correction/retraction notices, actual review status, study scope and truthful product availability. Explain their effect on the reader's interpretation in plain language. Private workflow directions belong in the gap log. A draft may explain that a full-text source is missing without publishing internal packet codes or the owner's interests.

## Acceptance

Inspect a complete drug original-use and condition-specific mechanism path, its sidebar/deep link and source locators on desktop/mobile. Check a primary mechanistic experiment, context-only rationale, missing mechanism evidence and an unsupported clinical inference: the last must not become a benefit claim. Reconcile requirements to prospective fetch fields and gap records without rewriting historical receipts. Check both initial generated pages and unchanged reruns.

Inspect visible navigation/product/article/PDF text and actual delivered HTML/hydration/API/search data for private preference/selection/process fields. Retain governance provenance in private artifacts. Test a configured owner-priority candidate remains accessible under a neutral topic link while its private rationale is absent; retain a meaningful correction/uncertainty notice as a counterexample to indiscriminate removal. Record exact code/export/deployment hashes and unresolved inputs. Rule completion, interface completion and scientific release remain separate.
