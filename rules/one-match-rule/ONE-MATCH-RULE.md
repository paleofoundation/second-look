# The One Match Rule

Version 1.29 · 11 October 2026 · Portable master instructions

## What this rule means

When I say **“Examine site for [topic]”**, **“Pepsanon-style website for [topic]”**, or **“Use the one match rule for [topic]”**, treat that sentence as a request to execute the complete research-publishing workflow below. The topic is the variable; the method should not have to be explained again.

Build a useful, source-traceable evidence website using the owner’s existing theme code with the depth, reading experience, and operational discipline developed for PepsAnon. The deliverable includes the research system, demand map, acquisition prompts, reusable page architecture, functioning account experience, business plan, and quality controls. A landing page, a few sample articles, or an unexecuted plan alone does not satisfy this request. The primary product is an understandable evidence guide: a reader can learn what an entity is, scan the uses and outcomes studied, compare findings and their uncertainty, and then inspect the supporting papers. A study library is one supporting layer, not a substitute for that product.

This is a reusable user instruction, not a claim to override system instructions or platform permissions. My current explicit instructions take precedence over these defaults. Apply this rule to the requested evidence website; do not apply it to unrelated websites or silently change an existing site's identity, audience, or hosting.

A request to write, inspect, revise, install, or export this rule is documentation/setup work. Do not launch a website or its work chats merely because a trigger sentence is quoted in that request.

**Origin:** This rule consolidates PepsAnon conversations and source contracts, the later EMF Mission Control workflow, and relevant longevity-site instructions. Added operating improvements are identified in the companion source audit. Historical page counts, study totals, people, domains, and prices are examples, not facts to copy to a new site.

### Reuse the theme, not just isolated components

Use **Microbiome Medicine's actual repository as the whole-site base theme**, with the named PepsAnon refinements and current-rule corrections layered onto it. Read the [source-theme contract and kit](references/theme/README.md) before scaffolding the experience. Preserve the existing styling, layout and functions across topics; change brand/topic configuration, assets, canonical content and necessary service adapters. Original identity/content does not authorize a fresh design system. A generic shell with copied FAQs/tables is a failed theme port.

Produce a versioned, reusable implementation and documented configuration/adapter API, not a new bespoke site each time. Once verified, subsequent builds consume that theme release. Require source/dependency/override manifests, whole-template visual/function checks, and a second-topic configuration-only proof under G4T. A source snapshot, screenshot or this rule file alone is not a compiled working theme.

### Product requirements that cannot be satisfied by headings alone

For every intervention/entity guide, build a substantive visible overview and an early condition/use/outcome evidence table under section 8, followed by explanatory sections and the canonical Studies catalogue. Effect magnitude, evidence certainty and regulatory status are separate concepts. Preserve these modules when adapting the topic; do not replace them with lists of papers, administrative status text or links to other pages. Validate one complete representative reader journey before accepting or multiplying the template. Technical checks and a private preview do not establish that the requested evidence website is complete.

Applicable guides also require a populated, page-specific FAQ module using the Microbiome Medicine handling, and PepsAnon-derived evidence-table styling and right-side research overlays. The hero author block and persistent context sidebar are separate from that overlay. Read the reference reuse contract in section 8 and the packaged [code references](references/reading-ui/README.md); do not substitute an unrelated accordion, generic table or full-page study link and call it equivalent.

Every cited research paper also requires a dedicated, substantive review answering Q01–Q15. Plan its source inputs before acquisition; do not discover after building the site that only citation metadata was collected. Full reviews use the current access policy: PepsAnon, Second Look and new builds use free authenticated access with first-login reader-role intake until a billing transition is explicitly configured and verified. Sections 8, 10 and 11 define review completeness, intake and actual server-enforced protection.

## 1. Start from one sentence

Infer and record the topic, likely audience, central reader decisions, domain boundaries, available source material, and a working site name. Make reasonable reversible decisions and begin. Do not make me fill in a questionnaire about colors, menus, post types, study fields, or monetization before doing useful work.

Default audience: curious, informed non-specialists, with deeper methods and source detail available to clinicians and researchers. Explain unfamiliar terms without diluting scientific meaning.

Preserve the requested subject breadth for a single site as well as a portfolio. A drug-repurposing request does not become a Long COVID-only publication merely because those papers are available first. Record the full intended scope, the evidence-backed initial slice and the expansion backlog separately. Label a narrow guide by that scope. On a parent entity page, distinguish original/established uses, investigated uses, and questions not yet assessed using sources permitted by the task. Never invent a comprehensive use list or imply that a selected collection covers the whole literature.

**Parent scope cannot inherit a narrow pilot by accident.** A named entity parent must stay about that entity across its overview, Benefits, Safety, Mechanisms, Results, FAQs and related navigation. Appending several condition rows to a one-condition article does not establish breadth. Keep scoped condition questions as child answers and build the parent from a versioned entity–condition–outcome coverage map, with source-supported context and meaningful synthesis. A narrow available corpus creates explicit research gaps, not permission to invent broader claims or call a narrow parent finished. Read the [topic-scope and directory contract](references/topic-scope-and-directories.md); preserve this mapping through ingestion/projection/generation.

Record explicit entity and condition coverage targets independently. A request for both cannot be fulfilled by the same number of papers, questions or entity–condition pairs. Keep distinct target registries and a linked coverage matrix; preserve unfinished targets through batch handoffs. The drug-repurposing profile in Appendix C requires an initial portfolio of 30 distinct drugs and 30 distinct conditions. These counts are scoped to that profile, not defaults for every subject.

When the request concerns a portfolio of scientific fields, distinguish a publication's field from the narrower conditions, interventions and reader questions within it. Preserve the owner's chosen field names and scope; do not substitute a single symptom or life stage for an entire requested field. Map overlap between proposed and existing publications before creating a duplicate. A recommendation to combine fields does not authorize merging, renaming or retiring a site.

Default first priorities:

1. Establish Mission Control and a durable project brief; resolve the reusable theme release or pinned Microbiome source and PepsAnon overlays before dependent layout work.
2. Discover and organize the real questions people ask.
3. Write and persist the ingestion plan before the first ingest and before the first file request goes to Grokbot; complete the planning check in section 4.
4. Produce a filled, copy-ready Grokbot acquisition prompt tied to that plan early.
5. Build and inspect one representative entity → condition/outcome → synthesis → study/source journey while evidence collection runs; use it to validate the shared page contract before scaling content.
6. Ingest, verify, synthesize, and publish only the declared scope that passes its gates.
7. Deliver the account experience, monetization plan, and maintainable update process.

If the name, palette, author, scope, deployment audience, merchant, or source access is already known, reuse that information accurately. Choose a provisional name and two-color brand when they are absent. Leave authorship unassigned until a real assignment is known. Do not invent credentials, biography, ORCID, domain ownership, a medical reviewer, or investigator approval.

Use the user's requested model/settings where available; do not bake a model name or unsupported speed promise into this rule. State meaningful assumptions briefly. Ask only for information that materially blocks work, and continue independent work while waiting. A missing merchant account does not block research, design, or the business plan.

### Required first handoff

Before a large build becomes opaque, show the working name, audience, scope and separate target counts, main/accent/pale-accent colors and homepage illustration direction, resolved theme source/release and adaptation scope, planned post types, first research priorities, Mission Control status, and the saved ingestion plan with its readiness status. Identify the actual Grokbot input attachment/location and planned return mechanism alongside the prompt. Provide the filled Grokbot prompt or its location only after the plan passes its planning check. Distinguish work already executed from work merely commissioned. Give a realistic scope-based estimate; a large literature review is not a five-minute website task.

### Understand existing validation methods before intervention work

When a target already has domain-specific validation protocols or intervention guidelines, reconcile those methods before identifying or classifying new interventions under them and before writing intervention-based user guides. Locate the authoritative sources, their version or content hash, the actual site/source binding, and any implementation or worked examples. A related redesign preview is not automatically the canonical site.

Write a versioned method contract translating the resolved rules into explicit criteria: relevant evidence units and context matching, required inputs, classifications, scoring or thresholds where actually specified, missing-data treatment and adjudication. Preserve exact source locators and unresolved decisions. Check the interpretation against representative existing examples and their underlying sources, including insufficient or contradictory evidence. Reading a protocol page or finding an existing status badge is not proof that its interpretation is correct or that clinical efficacy has been established.

Do not invent absent weights, cutoffs, protocol versions or status meanings, replace the owner's framework with a generic ranking, or treat a research-priority designation, prediction or mechanistic change as established patient benefit. Hold only the dependent intervention identification/classification and guide content when an authoritative rule remains unresolved; continue protocol/source inventory, reader-question discovery, guide structure and other independent work. Record these dependencies in the ingestion plan so requests collect the information the actual method needs. This requirement does not waive GP Plan or create a routine extra owner-approval step; seek owner input only for genuinely missing authoritative information or a decision reserved to them.

## 2. Mission Control and work chips

Use the current coordinating chat as **[Site] — Mission Control**. If the environment can create a separate coordinating chat and that is requested, create it and link it. Do not duplicate an existing controller. Maintain a durable `mission-control.json` or equivalent board that survives chat context loss.

Mission Control owns scope, priorities, contracts, dependency order, acceptance, integration, and release. Workers return evidence; they do not declare their own acceptance or independently publish a competing version. One integrator owns the canonical site checkout and deployment.

Prepare distinct work chats/packets for:

| Workstream | Required result | May start when |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Reddit corpus, question clusters, priority map, exclusions | Scope is drafted |
| Literature | Filled Grokbot brief tied to the ingestion plan, primary/context packs, access gaps | Discovery/planning may start immediately; no Grokbot file-request handoff or dispatch before the persisted plan passes its planning check |
| Ingest | Validated canonical records, identity map, extraction provenance | Plan/schema work immediately; first extraction or import only after the persisted plan passes its planning check and the received packet is reconciled |
| Experience | Reusable source-derived theme, configuration/adapters, navigation, post shells, sidebars and responsive behavior | Post-type contract and accessible pinned theme/source, dependency inventory and overlay map exist |
| Answers | Cited guides, FAQs, evidence synthesis, glossary links | Relevant question and evidence records are accepted |
| Study reviews | Universal-question appraisals, citation previews, full reviews, claim links and review versions | Source identity/access reconciled; calibrate diverse examples before scaling |
| Accounts | Real login, private saved library, notes/preferences where supported | Identity and persistence path is verified |
| Business | Audience/offers, guide product, launch economics, fulfillment plan | Initial demand map exists |
| Review | Independent source, content, UX, account, and release checks | Relevant deliverables exist |

For large topics, split Demand and Literature into bounded topic batches under stable IDs, with one shared schema. Do not create dozens of nearly identical chats before they have useful work.

Where the host supports task creation and the user has authorized those work chats, create them and return their actual links. Otherwise, emit clickable follow-up chips that explicitly request a separate task, with a self-contained prompt or a real attached packet. In Codex, use supported artifact follow-up chips. In other hosts, use their native equivalent or clearly labeled copyable prompts. A displayed chip is an invitation; it is not a running task. Never claim a chat, automation, background worker, or Grokbot job exists without a successful operation.

Use subagents for independent work during the active build when available. Code workers use isolated checkouts or nonoverlapping ownership. Research workers write their own output batches. All workers receive the current shared contract, source version, output location, prerequisites, acceptance criteria, and return destination.

### Worker packet contract

Every packet contains: site/topic; exact task; scope IDs; input references accessible to the worker; source version; allowed output location; dependencies; concrete deliverables; evidence requirements; checks; stop conditions; and return instructions. Never rely on a worker remembering another chat.

Every handoff contains: completed scope, artifact links/paths, source/code version, criteria-to-evidence mapping, tests and results, unresolved gaps, conflicts, and a proposed status. Mission Control records its own dated **PASS**, **FAIL**, or **HOLD** with reasons. HOLD means missing work/prerequisite; FAIL means an observed defect; PASS applies only to the inspected scope and version. Preserve prior decisions. Any relevant artifact change invalidates its dependent acceptance until rechecked.

### Requested recurring runs across multiple fields

When the owner requests recurring One Match work across selected targets, keep a portfolio registry mapping every target to its existing site, source, controller, scope, method contract, adopted rule version, checkpoint and next action. Locate existing work first; initialize a new build only when no matching project exists. Preserve each site's identity, audience, release policy and any frozen pilot baseline. Record subsequent rule adoption separately.

On each scheduled run, account for every selected target and advance its next authorized actionable work from the checkpoint. Resume unfinished acquisition, ingestion, appraisal, guides or implementation and import new evidence idempotently; do not recreate sites or restart completed research each week. Record what was done, unchanged, deferred or held and why. A status check alone does not establish completion, and a quiet evidence week does not require fabricated changes. Respect active builders and single-integrator ownership. Use the existing matching schedule through the host's supported automation tools; a schedule request does not authorize duplicate controllers, new messaging, spending or broader publication access. Frequency, target list and time are owner/project settings, not universal defaults.

## 3. Find the questions before choosing the articles

Reddit supplies demand signals and reader language. Scientific sources supply the answers. A popular claim is not evidence of efficacy, harm, causality, sales volume, or prevalence.

Create a real question-discovery protocol, not a paragraph saying “research Reddit.” Start with broad topic searches and reader decisions, then expand neutral synonyms, concerns, misconceptions, comparison questions, and community vocabulary. Include both enthusiastic and skeptical communities, general-interest communities, and relevant specialist communities. Record inaccessible sources and sampling bias.

Use a declared recent window, normally the previous 24 months, plus a separately reported historical window. Record the actual execution date, cutoff, exact queries, search surface/API, filters, sort, returned URLs, and zero results. Use permitted public access; do not bypass access blocks.

For retained threads record canonical URL/ID, subreddit, actual post date if available, retrieval date, access status, paraphrased question, decision context, query family, topic IDs, and duplicate/crosspost cluster. Store engagement only when observed; unknown values remain null. Do not collect usernames or create health profiles. Avoid copying entire discussions.

Exclude or separately label vendor promotion, recruitment, bots when evident, affiliate material, unrelated homonyms, duplicates, and removed posts without recoverable questions. Keep an exclusion log. Missing metadata must not silently turn a search snippet into a verified thread.

Cluster questions by what a reader needs to decide. Distinguish intervention, condition, outcome, population, formulation/route, comparison, safety, interaction, quality, cost/access, and practical use. Keep overlapping clusters linked rather than counting them as unique people.

### Priority method

Aim for **40 meaningful question clusters** by default. For a narrow topic, deliver fewer with an explanation rather than inventing demand. This is an editorial research target, not a claim of Reddit-wide “top 40.” PepsAnon's 30 peptide and 30 stack pages are a topic-specific precedent, not a universal page quota.

Use a transparent reproducible initial score: `2 × independent question-thread clusters + distinct relevant subreddits`. Report recent and historical counts separately. Validate the top priorities by direct inspection where possible. Compare the ranking after excluding historical threads and after removing the largest contributing subreddit. Keep urgency, unmet evidence need, and editorial importance in separate fields rather than secretly altering the popularity score.

For a topic labeled demand-validated, seek at least two independent question-thread clusters and two communities, or record a justified niche exception. A vital safety topic may be included as an editorial priority without pretending it is popular. If access is poor, mark the ranking provisional and retain the exact evidence available.

For each topic produce: stable ID; neutral user question; reader decision; source links; demand status and counts; synonyms; proposed page type; intended canonical page/section; related topics; scientific scope; paper-search brief; direct/indirect evidence needs; and current answer status. Maintain a coverage matrix from question → search brief → records → claims → page/FAQ → product opportunity.

For acquisition, turn the observed high-priority questions into specific source requests. Every priority question must link to its actual Reddit-demand records (or an explicitly separate editorial rationale), applicable entity and condition IDs, the reader decision, population/comparator/outcomes, retrieval queries, expected source types and destination overview/table/FAQ. Track unanswered and unsearched questions explicitly. Preserve explicit owner-required candidates and questions in a separately labeled priority field even when they do not rank highly in the observed Reddit sample. Map them into the appropriate retrieval briefs and page coverage; do not fabricate Reddit demand to justify them or silently drop them during shortlist selection. An owner-required target is a research obligation, not a claim of efficacy. Scientific sources must support the answers; Reddit identifies what needs answering. Generic entity searches and bulk paper counts do not establish that the fetch addresses the question map.

Continue open discovery until two successive bounded batches add no material question category, or a declared access/time limit is reached. Report within-protocol saturation or incomplete discovery accurately. Do not delay all work indefinitely seeking impossible proof of exhaustiveness.

Public community collections show useful questions, concise research focus, relevant guides, and original discussion links. Keep scoring, owner preferences, internal priorities, worker instructions and research-management fields in private Mission Control. Build an explicit reader-safe projection rather than serializing private selection records into page props, APIs, search documents or generated files. Label community links **Discussion** and keep them out of primary-study counts.

## 4. Give Grokbot an executable literature assignment

### Ingestion plan comes before requests and ingestion

**A concrete ingestion plan must exist before the first ingest and before the first file request goes out to Grokbot.** Persist it as `ingest-plan.md` or an equivalent durable, accessible project artifact. An intention, an empty template, a Grokbot prompt by itself, or scattered instructions do not satisfy this requirement. This is a prerequisite for handing a request to the user as ready to send as well as for dispatching it through any tool. It also applies to scoping acquisition, calibration batches, direct worker acquisition and supplied-file ingestion; none is an exception to planning first.

Mission Control writes and checks the plan autonomously; this requirement does not introduce a new owner-approval step. The plan must specify:

- Scope and intended reader questions, inclusion/exclusion rules, discovery versus closed-packet mode, PRIMARY/CONTEXT separation, and known gaps.
- Any existing validation-method contract, required inputs and unresolved method dependencies that constrain intervention identification, classification or guide content.
- What files and metadata are requested, the actual versioned delivery/extraction schema, naming/manifest/hash conventions, and the complete location contract below: worker-readable inputs, named source-search/full-text locations, delivery method and receiver-accessible packet layout. Distinguish planned output names from files already delivered.
- Separate entity and condition target registries where requested, their demand-question links, selection rationale, pair/coverage matrix and batches that together address the full declared portfolio. Include the reverse ChatGPT ingest-prompt deliverable and its receipt checks.
- Mechanism-of-action inputs for the original use and each investigated condition: target/pathway/action, proposed causal chain and condition relevance, model/species/tissue, exposure/concentration/duration, directly tested versus source-proposed or inferred steps, contradictory findings/alternative explanations, clinical translation limits and exact claim/numerical source locators. Keep primary mechanistic experiments distinct from reviews/textbooks and clinical outcome evidence; record missing inputs. Map this schema to the shared Mechanisms section before requests/ingest.
- Canonical identities, deduplication and study-family handling; provenance and result/claim/page relationships.
- Batch priorities and dependencies, responsible workers/integrator, calibration approach and criteria for scaling; estimate workload from observed throughput when available.
- Extraction and numerical/source verification, review boundaries, acceptance checks, count reconciliation and treatment of missing/unreadable/contradictory material.
- Retry/resume, idempotent reimport, preservation of reviewed work, recovery/rollback and revision handling.
- How accepted batches become the entity overview, condition/use/outcome synthesis rows, Mechanism of Action sections, explanatory prose, reviews, FAQs and citations; their shared typed data and renderer; per-batch completion evidence and unresolved-work reporting. Include lawful context sources for entity identity, original uses and regulatory/safety context when acquisition is permitted. In a closed packet, retain source restrictions and record those context gaps rather than filling them from memory.
- A versioned review-input map covering Q01–Q15 for every research paper intended for citation, including secondary research. Carry the actual question definitions, required fields, source/locator requirements and paper→review→page relationships in the worker packet. Request lawful full texts, supplements, protocols/registrations, statistical methods and correction/version notices where relevant; record unavailable inputs. Separate reported extraction from editorial appraisal and human approval. Also assign account/intake implementation to its own workstream; Grokbot does not collect readers' personal data.

Record the plan ID/version, creation time, artifact hash or immutable version, location and readiness decision in Mission Control. Check that the plan and schema are accessible and usable for the requested scope before marking **GP Plan PASS**. Every outgoing acquisition request and ingest batch references that plan version. Missing, placeholder-only or materially mismatched plans mean HOLD for those operations; continue independent discovery, design, schema and account work. Internal drafting of a prompt is allowed, but it is not a ready-to-send handoff.

For review readiness, use the portable [review and intake contract](references/research-reviews/README.md) and machine-readable question/input map. Every required reader-facing field must have a planned source or responsible authoring step and a missing-data state. At receipt, reconcile each cited-paper candidate against Q01–Q15 inputs; return a question-level extraction/gap ledger, not just PDFs and abstracts. Mark work not extracted differently from a field genuinely not reported in a fully inspected source. The integrator supplies design-appropriate interpretation and the human reviewer supplies any required approval; worker extraction is neither. New citations, missing supplements or source corrections reopen affected reviews and downstream acceptance. This closes the acquisition-to-review loop without manufacturing unavailable evidence or overriding closed-packet limits.

Also map the [evidence exploration inputs](references/evidence-exploration.md) into that same plan/schema: outcome definitions/aliases and clinical role, meaningful-change basis or missingness, comparison context, overlap-aware counts and interaction evidence/severity/coverage. Distinguish reported data from editorial interpretation and runtime filter state; Grokbot must not invent appraisals or classifications. Reuse accepted records and acquire only verified missing inputs under the source restrictions.

For a broad subject, the initial plan can cover a bounded scoping acquisition with explicit unknowns and a method for resolving them; do not invent final candidates or counts. Update it when candidates, scope or schema change, and recheck the affected request/batch before proceeding. Existing or unsolicited files may be retained and inventoried in raw staging while the plan is prepared; do not start substantive extraction or canonical import first. If a running project already requested or ingested files without a plan, record the actual chronology and reconcile the plan before the next dependent operation; never backdate compliance.

### Acquisition prompt

After GP Plan passes, generate a fully populated version of the prompt in Appendix A referencing the saved plan and schema. Fill the topic, current dates, scope, topic IDs, synonyms, exclusions, output schema, and topic-by-topic retrieval briefs. Do not hand me a generic prompt full of placeholders I must design myself. Provide it early enough that my Grokbot can work while the site is being built.

For a broad subject without defensible specific candidates yet, use neutral seed questions and an explicitly labeled **scoping acquisition brief**, covered by the saved ingestion plan and GP Plan PASS before handoff. That brief must tell Grokbot how to discover candidate entity–question pairs and return the source-backed shortlist; it must not invent a list of popular or effective drugs. In the same workflow, turn the observed candidates and demand map into the full topic-specific retrieval packet, preserving IDs and logging scope and plan revisions. Initial scoping is an intermediate handoff, not completion of the final acquisition brief. Continue design, schema and account work while this dependency resolves.

### File locations and the return to ChatGPT

Every copy-ready Grokbot request must contain a filled **Where to look / Where to return** block. Naming “research papers” or an unattached local folder is insufficient. Resolve the actual environment and existing project assets; do not make the owner design the transfer process again.

1. **Input packet:** identify the exact attachment/archive name or authorized share URL/object ID that Grokbot can access, plus the relative paths to the ingestion plan, schema, topic/target/question maps, existing source inventory and packet rules. State whether existing full texts are attached, available in a named connected folder, or not supplied. A filesystem path is valid only for a worker with verified access to that filesystem. A path on ChatGPT's or the owner's computer does not by itself give Grok access; a Grok-local path will not by itself give ChatGPT access.
2. **Places to search and obtain files:** name the domain-relevant indexes, registries and lawful full-text repositories with entry URLs; supply the actual question-specific search terms/queries and verified known DOI/PMID/publisher links when available. Say which sources identify papers and which can supply full texts/supplements. Specify the retrieval order and authorized library/repository options, including source restrictions. Search beyond open-access collections for eligible evidence; keep paywalled/missing files in the gap log. A database landing page or abstract is not an acquired full text. In closed-packet mode list the exact allowed packet/files instead and prohibit external retrieval as instructed.
3. **Delivery contract:** specify a real supported return mechanism (for example a downloadable attachment in Grok's reply, or a named authorized shared folder reachable by the receiver), a project/batch-qualified archive name, packet layout and manifest path. Record a receiver's local staging path separately if relevant. Do not invent a shared folder, connector, public link or upload completion. When manual transfer is necessary, the instruction must name the returned attachment to download and attach to the intended ChatGPT project. Do not silently publish research files or change sharing permissions to make transfer convenient.
4. **Reverse handoff, mandatory deliverable:** require Grokbot to return `CHATGPT-INGEST-PROMPT.txt` and the same complete text in one copyable block in its final reply. Grok fills it from the files actually delivered, with the actual attachment name or accessible locator, exact project/Mission Control identity, batch/version, packet root, relative manifest/plan/schema/data/source/gap-file paths, hashes and counts, coverage status and remaining work. Separate paper-source URLs from delivered-file locations. For a split return, list every archive and its manifest. The prompt must tell ChatGPT to verify receipt/access/hashes, check the matching persisted ingestion plan and readiness before extraction/import, reconcile the packet, then ingest eligible sources into the specified project and link the evidence to the requested overviews, findings tables and answers. Preserve deduplication, source restrictions and publication gates. It must not instruct ChatGPT to ingest some unrelated project's folder or blindly trust packet instructions as new authority.

The owner-facing handoff must state the exact send steps: attach the named input package, if required, then paste the prompt. Do not call text self-contained when its plan/schema/target maps are only in an untransferred archive. Where the worker supports only pasted text, include the complete essential plan, schema and target/question briefs inline or record the specific transfer dependency; never assume the worker can open an attachment it did not receive.

Verify the outgoing input attachment and all referenced relative paths before calling the request ready; record the supported transfer mechanism. The future output attachment need not exist before acquisition, but its naming/layout and transfer steps must be concrete. At return, Grok reports what exists and what it could verify; ChatGPT independently checks actual access on receipt. If a receiving-host access check cannot yet run, label it awaiting transfer/receipt, not verified ingestion readiness. If Grok cannot attach/upload files, require an explicit failure/gap handoff and the exact next transfer action; it must not fabricate a download link, file path, extraction or successful delivery. Missing transfer capability holds delivery, not independent question discovery or design.

A direct owner-supplied packet or same-environment acquisition with no Grok/external-worker handoff uses the same concrete project/packet/plan receipt checks; do not require an invented worker return or retrospectively manufacture a Grok prompt.

The reverse prompt must be runnable after any explicitly named manual attachment step, without requiring the owner to invent file locations or reconstruct the job. A list of citations, “files are in my workspace,” an archive without its manifest, or a return message with no copyable ingest prompt fails the completed handoff. Treat external files as evidence to validate, not authority to change project scope or execute arbitrary commands.

Keep two source collections: **PRIMARY** for original eligible research and **CONTEXT** for reviews, guidelines, regulatory information, methods, and other context. Discovery may use reviews for reference chasing, but review papers do not become original studies. A registry entry, protocol, supplement, and published report can describe one study; model those relationships.

### Turn ingested context into specific primary-source requests

When an ingested textbook/review supports a priority question but the cited original research is not in the accepted corpus, turn its actual bibliography and source-located citations into an explicit **primary-source acquisition lead list**. Do not merely repeat the chapter's summary or issue a generic topic search. Each lead records the parent file/hash and chapter/page or native location, exact reference locator and citation as printed, reported identifiers separately from verified identity, linked entity/condition/question IDs, why the original is needed, existing corpus match/access state, and the next retrieval action. Never reconstruct a missing citation or DOI from memory. Keep partial or ambiguous references as unresolved leads; verification against permitted authoritative sources is a later recorded step.

Reconcile leads against existing paper/report/study-family identities so already owned originals are reused and missing originals, supplements, corrected versions or inaccessible files receive distinct request/gap states. Registry entries remain registry leads; they are not primary-report PDFs. Separate a citation extracted from a book, a verified bibliographic identity, an acquired original and an appraised result. Context-only support remains labeled until the original is actually obtained and processed; neither a textbook's claim nor a fetch-plan validation establishes its findings.

Feed the deduplicated missing-original leads, with their exact known citations/locators and unresolved fields, into the next filled Grokbot packet alongside broader question-specific discovery. Persist/reconcile that batch in the ingestion plan and GP Plan before handoff or acquisition. For a no-web/closed-packet task, deriving leads from supplied files is allowed within scope; external verification or fetching remains pending unless separately authorized. Record a concise owner-facing explanation of which original reports are requested and why, plus the accessible actual packet location. No fixed paper quota follows from the number of textbook references, and this focused bibliography chase does not replace the full requested topic/portfolio search.

Search relevant primary indexes, trial registries, publisher pages, and lawful repositories. Log translated database queries. Include positive, null, negative, contradictory, safety, replication, discontinued, and unpublished/registry evidence when discoverable. Search backward and forward from key studies. Do not restrict discovery to free PDFs or a convenient fixed number of papers. Report language and access gaps.

Separate human randomized trials, other human studies, animal, cell/ex-vivo, engineering/modeling, preprints, and secondary context. Preserve intervention identity, route/formulation, dose/concentration, duration, comparator, population, outcome, and timing. Component evidence is not evidence for a combination. In-silico plausibility is not clinical efficacy.

Maintain two independent status dimensions:

- **Processing:** discovered → identity checked → abstract read → full text acquired → extracted → audited → synthesized → published.
- **Scientific assessment:** not appraised, then documented outcome-specific certainty/risk-of-bias judgments where actually performed.

Also track editorial/human review separately. A named investigator is not a completed review. A full-text PDF is not a completed appraisal. A passed import is not a medical endorsement.

## 5. Ingest evidence without losing identity or provenance

Before the first substantive extraction or import, verify the persisted ingestion plan and GP Plan PASS from section 4 against the received packet and current schema. Update and recheck material mismatches first. This applies to closed packets and calibration batches as well as large imports; file availability does not waive the prerequisite.

Inspect the incoming archive/manifest before changing public content. Keep raw source files immutable, compute hashes, preserve filenames, and store normalized outputs separately. Do not add a redundant “What is [entity]?” subheading beneath it; preserve section anchors and any genuinely distinct questions in their appropriate modules. Enforce this in the shared renderer so regeneration cannot restore the unwanted heading.

The overview explains what the entity is, its original/established role where verified, why other uses are being investigated, what the declared evidence supports or leaves uncertain, and the most consequential applicability/safety boundary. Keep detailed condition questions explicitly scoped and linked, and reconcile all available eligible syntheses plus unresolved condition coverage through one canonical adapter; unrelated page titles, candidate links and directory counts cannot supply missing substance. It must orient a reader unfamiliar with the entity. Describing how the site's files were collected is not an overview. Use cited context and synthesis; never invent missing regulatory or clinical facts. If the permitted sources cannot establish necessary context, identify that precise gap and retain the unfinished status.

Every intervention guide requires a visible condition/use/outcome table. Each row is an editorial synthesis of a specific reader question, not a paper title or an ingestion record. Multiple rows may cover one condition when outcomes, prevention versus treatment, populations or timepoints differ. Do not require a fixed number of rows or grade categories; display the source-supported scope and its gaps.

| Row element | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Condition and purpose | What use is being considered; distinguish original/established use, repurposing hypothesis, prevention and treatment without inferring approval |
| Population and comparison | Relevant humans/animals/cells, formulation/route, comparator, outcome and follow-up; expose consequential mismatches in the visible row or its immediate detail |
| Finding | Plain-language direction and bounded takeaway: benefit, harm, no important effect when supported, mixed or inconclusive; preserve distinction from no eligible evidence |
| Effect magnitude | Source-verified numbers, units, absolute contrast and uncertainty where available; relative estimates with their proper context; do not silently pool incompatible outcomes |
| Confidence in evidence | Documented outcome-specific certainty, with method/version and rationale; keep “Not appraised” distinct from Low |
| Evidence base | Independent study families and design, relevant participants where meaningful, human versus preclinical and direct versus indirect support; background reviews/textbooks are not additional primary results |
| Main limitation | The limitation most likely to change interpretation or applicability; include missing/full-text/retraction concerns when relevant |
| Traceability and state | Links to the scoped answer, material results, supporting study reviews and original sources; synthesis version/search cutoff, review state and explicit pending work |

Regulatory approval is a separate dated, jurisdiction- and indication-specific dimension. A treatment appearing in this table is not a recommendation or evidence of approval. Keep safety findings and important contrary/null findings visible, and avoid ranking unlike outcomes on a single drug-wide score.

Keep **measured direction** (increase/decrease/mixed/no detected change) separate from **clinical interpretation** (benefit/harm/context-dependent/uncertain). An upward arrow is not automatically good; statistical significance is not clinical importance, and an imprecise null does not establish no important effect. Preserve endpoint definitions, time horizons, uncertainty and supported meaningful-change thresholds. A finding on one symptom or surrogate must not become an overall condition-benefit badge. Any condition-level summary must identify the decision-relevant outcomes and its synthesis method; when those inputs are absent, show the scoped outcome findings and the limitation rather than inventing a composite score.

For cross-intervention comparisons, align indication, population, formulation/route, comparator, endpoint definition and time horizon. Explicitly distinguish direct head-to-head evidence, separate trials against other controls and a formally performed indirect synthesis. Preserve a **not directly comparable** state. Side-by-side values, separate significance tests, larger study counts or different certainty labels do not establish superiority. Indirect/network comparisons require documented methods, assumptions and appropriate review. Do not copy Examine's combined efficacy letter grades into the separate certainty/magnitude model or label them formal GRADE.

**High / Moderate / Low must say what they measure.** For the simplified reader display, use these labels for evidence certainty only after a documented appraisal; retain the full underlying categories and rationale, including Very low where the adopted method uses it. Do not automatically convert “Not appraised,” “No eligible evidence in the declared search,” or “Preclinical only” into Low. The explicitly adopted selected-source editorial profile below permits a completed, source-backed Low judgment about clinical inference from nonhuman evidence only while retaining its explicit nonhuman state; it never manufactures human clinical evidence. If effect magnitude is also classified as High / Moderate / Low, give it a separate “Effect magnitude” column with an outcome-specific, sourced or justified threshold, units and time horizon. Where no defensible threshold exists, show the numerical estimate and “Magnitude not classified.” Never derive magnitude from statistical significance, study count, certainty or a generic percentage cutoff. Do not label this display formal GRADE unless that method was actually completed.

### Selected-source editorial outcome grading

For PepsAnon-style grading explicitly adopted by Second Look, complete a **selected-source editorial assessment** for each eligible outcome using the [versioned grading contract](references/evidence-grading.md). Assess the actual source-backed finding, exact intervention/population/comparison/outcome/timepoint scope and all five domains—Study methods, Directness, Precision, Consistency and Source integrity—with a concise rationale, source IDs, source locators and actual assessment date. High/Moderate/Low measure confidence in that specific finding independently of benefit, null or harm direction; do not infer grades from a design label, P value, study count or missing workflow status. Available abstracts may support a limited judgment with access and unknown methods stated, without pretending a full-text review was completed. An unresolved correction permits only an explicit Low editorial judgment with the affected conclusion still held; quarantined/retracted reports cannot support a grade. Computational-only evidence can support a source-explained Low clinical-inference judgment only with its nonhuman status prominent, never an automatic unknown-to-Low fallback.

No eligible evidence remains truthful empty coverage, not a manufactured graded outcome. This scoped editorial assessment is AI-assisted when that is how it was prepared; it is **not formal GRADE, a completed Q01–Q15 study review, independent clinical review or publication approval**. The broader review, source-verification, access and exact-version release obligations in this rule remain unchanged.

A pending or uncertain outcome still gets a row with a concrete reason. “We have not synthesized these available studies” is unfinished work; “the completed declared review remains inconclusive” is a substantive finding. An all-pending table can be an honest structural draft but cannot satisfy finished-guide acceptance. Restrict conclusions to what was verified and continue the authorized appraisal/synthesis work; do not fill grades to make the design look complete.

**Reader-facing confidence must explain the finding.** Internal `not_appraised` is a valid workflow state, not a sufficient answer to “How confident should I be?” A cell containing only “Not appraised” and “Magnitude is separate from certainty” fails G4R. Retain the honest state in the data, but render a concise, outcome-specific account of what can and cannot be concluded and why: for example, the correction is unresolved, only an abstract is available, estimates remain imprecise, or available evidence is indirect. Use only documented limitations and make clear when the assessment itself remains unfinished. An actual completed outcome appraisal may supply High/Moderate/Low/Very low certainty and its reason; never manufacture that appraisal by renaming unknown as Low, Insufficient or a completed “inconclusive” verdict. Move the general magnitude-versus-certainty explanation to one useful legend/methods link, not repeated cell filler. Ensure text has readable spacing and hierarchy on desktop and mobile.

Prioritize completing the permissible source verification, outcome appraisal and synthesis behind those cells; copy changes alone do not complete the missing research. Track row-specific missing inputs, permitted next actions and review dependencies in the existing plan. Preserve an explicit owner restriction on new ingest or clinical release. Reconcile the same confidence explanation and review state across table, panel, comparison, FAQ and guide products. If evidence is incomplete, deliver useful supported findings and concrete limits while reporting that the evidence guide remains partial. This is an implementation requirement, not permission to invent a rating or treat AI appraisal as human approval.

Provide a typed synthesis model and reusable table renderer; a paragraph-only section schema is insufficient. Version row → result → study-family → paper/review relationships and invalidate affected rows when source status changes. On mobile retain all decision-critical fields through readable stacked rows or accessible table behavior, not by hiding certainty, limits or source access. Keep the overview, principal findings table, decisive limitations and critical safety information accessible to the site's ordinary reader under its existing audience permissions; premium products may add deeper appraisal, not remove the basic requested guide.

**PepsAnon table implementation:** adapt its semantic `EvidenceTable`, the associated table/grade/citation styles, and `ResearchPanel` triggers together. Findings and limitations come before technical PDF metadata. Unreviewed papers show clearly labeled source excerpts/metadata, never invented summaries or inferred limitations.

### Citations and counts

Number citations separately for each page, starting at `[1]`, assigned by first appearance in rendered reading order. Reuse the same number when citing the same source again. When editing existing pages, preserve old citation/section destinations with redirects or aliases as needed.

A source-dependent phrase without final punctuation is followed by its superscript; never move a full stop after the citation or use internal source IDs as displayed labels. Verify that the source supports every clause; split composite claims when needed. Use precise citations for scientific claims. Display DOI once in the appropriate reference/record area rather than repeating the same metadata in many prose blocks. Keep secondary, regulatory, commercial-identity, and discussion sources visibly classified. Commercial material can establish a marketed name or composition; it cannot establish efficacy. Inspect superscripts in rendered prose and tables at phone widths so they do not collide, wrap awkwardly, or obscure values.

Calculate public counts from the canonical data. Distinguish papers/reports, unique study families, reviewed studies, references, PDF files, answered questions, and merely collected questions. Never hardcode PepsAnon's totals, call every PDF a study, or count one cohort repeatedly across reports. Clickable totals lead to the corresponding filtered collection.

The page's evidence snapshot must describe its actual scoped evidence: independent studies by design, synthesized versus pending coverage, and source/search/review dates. Show participant totals only when denominators, overlapping cohorts, repeated outcomes and meta-analysis overlap can be reconciled; otherwise omit the aggregate or state why it is unavailable. Do not sum row Ns or add a meta-analysis's participants to its included trials. Counts explain coverage, not efficacy or certainty. Reconcile snapshot, finder, table, panel and catalogue against the same versioned records.

Do not insert arbitrary evidence grades. A certainty label must refer to an outcome, population and intervention, have a documented method, and reflect completed appraisal. Otherwise retain the internal Not appraised state and render the outcome-specific explanation required above, with its actual incomplete status; a bare status badge or repeated method slogan is insufficient. Do not add repetitive grading banners to the homepage or unrelated areas. Keep methods accessible.

Use section 8's explicit certainty-versus-magnitude contract in the Evidence table. PepsAnon's simplified certainty display uses Low / Moderate / High for assessed outcomes, with rationale, original appraisal and scope retained. Do not repeat grade badges below prose, inside FAQs, and in previews. This display is not a claim of formal GRADE, and Low must not conceal the distinction between animal-only findings and human clinical evidence. Keep an explicit Not appraised state. A benefit assessment is not an overall safety rating.

Treat established contraindications, precautions, suspected reactions, theoretical concerns, and missing information separately. Trial exclusion criteria are not automatically clinical contraindications; unreported adverse events do not mean none occurred. Prioritize unresolved safety, contraindication, and interaction questions before polishing less consequential claims.

Interaction records and displays must separate confidence that an interaction exists from potential clinical severity. Preserve the resolved pair, population/route/exposure, evidence basis, source locators and assessment/check date. Distinguish theoretical support, unknown severity, unassessed or unmatched pairs, inaccessible sources and no finding within a declared search; none is a general clearance or claim of safety. Use a documented domain-appropriate assessment method rather than importing another product's thresholds. This requirement supplies evidence-based interaction information, not an automatically authorized personalized regimen or medication checker.

### Research reviews: one dedicated review for every cited paper

Build the reading path **consumer question → guide conclusion → cited paper's dedicated review → original source**. Every research paper cited anywhere in the site's reader-facing guides, FAQs, tables, bibliography or review prose must have a canonical review record and dedicated stable review page. This includes primary studies, research preprints, protocols, systematic reviews/meta-analyses and other scholarly research reviews—not only papers supporting favorable or major claims. A paper encountered only in a raw source's bibliography is an acquisition lead until the site itself cites it. Do not quietly relabel a cited review paper as CONTEXT to evade review; CONTEXT still prevents counting it as a new primary study. Regulatory pages, general web resources and book chapters retain appropriate source/context records; a textbook citation alone does not create a completed review of every original it mentions.

Deduplicate paper identities before assigning one canonical review route; retain separate reports from the same study with explicit family links. Reuse the same review across all citing pages. Maintain a generated cited-paper→review coverage ledger. Missing full text, incomplete extraction or pending appraisal requires a truthful pending review page and next action, not invented answers or removal of contrary citations. A pending page is a navigable status record, not a completed review. Every cited paper in a release declared review-complete must have its substantive review and applicable release checks completed; report the actual denominator and pending records. This requirement extends the older major-conclusion-only priority into universal cited-paper coverage. Prioritizing important papers remains a sequencing choice, not an exemption.

A review is an original source-traceable assessment, not a bibliographic record, copied abstract or fifteen empty headings. Make the exact result, decisive limitation, applicability to the reader's question and limits of the claim easy to inspect. “Examine-level” quality requires demonstrated comprehension, source navigation and review coverage; it is not established by branding or a populated library.

Use one versioned editorial review record to supply three depths: a concise citation preview (starting target 50–90 words), a main study review (starting target 500–800 words), and optional evidence details. These lengths are defaults from the new review plan, not hard quotas or replacements for the focused guide-section contract. A very small report may need less; a complex multi-arm trial may need more. Main reviews use clear grades 8–10 prose and the fixed navigation **Summary, Methods, Results, Limits, Sources**. Distinguish benefit results from harms and keep the main finding and decisive limitation visible before a membership boundary.

Every review answers the following internal questions, combining them into readable public sections rather than fifteen repetitive headings:

| ID | Universal study-review question | Required coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Q01 | What question was actually tested? | Objective, prespecification and the precise claim/outcome addressed |
| Q02 | Who or what was studied? | Human participants versus animals, isolated human tissue or cells; eligibility, setting and recruited/treated/analyzed counts |
| Q03 | What exactly was used? | Molecule/formulation, route, exposure, schedule, follow-up, product identity and co-interventions |
| Q04 | What was the comparator? | Control, allocation, randomization, blinding and timing; state their absence |
| Q05 | What was measured and does it matter? | Primary/secondary/exploratory outcome, instrument, timepoint, clinical versus surrogate endpoint and meaningful-change threshold if established |
| Q06 | How large was the result? | Each relevant arm, numerator/denominator, units, absolute contrast and uncertainty; within-group versus between-group distinction |
| Q07 | What happened to the other observations? | Attrition, missing outcomes, adherence, exclusions, analysis population and experimental unit; repeated measurements are not extra participants |
| Q08 | What harms were sought and found? | Ascertainment method, exposed denominator, follow-up, severity/events/withdrawals and detection limits |
| Q09 | How could the design or analysis mislead? | Applicable bias, confounding, selective reporting, multiplicity, precision and internal inconsistencies with source locators |
| Q10 | Does this apply to the reader or product? | Population, indication, molecule, formulation, route, exposure and endpoint matches/mismatches |
| Q11 | Is this independent evidence? | Cohort/study/experiment identity, overlapping reports, follow-ups and replication search scope |
| Q12 | Who conducted and supported it? | Authors, setting, funding, supplied products and disclosed conflicts without treating funding alone as proof of validity or invalidity |
| Q13 | Is the source current and intact? | Version/preprint status, access, supplements, protocol/registration, corrections/retractions, check dates and unfinished checks |
| Q14 | Which claims does it support or contradict? | Explicit guide/outcome/claim links and supported, indirect, contradictory or unassessed applicability; observed data versus authors' conclusions versus reviewer interpretation |
| Q15 | What should this change in our understanding? | Bounded takeaway, remaining unknowns and the future evidence that could change the guide, without individualized treatment instructions |

Retain the same Q01–Q15 IDs across study types, with explicit design-appropriate interpretation. For a review/meta-analysis assess its search/eligibility, included studies and populations, extraction/synthesis methods, overlap, heterogeneity, bias and applicability; do not invent a randomized comparator or primary participant count. A question may be not applicable or not identifiable only with a specific reason; unperformed extraction or appraisal remains pending. A completed declared investigation may conclude that a source did not report a field, but it cannot turn `not_checked`, `not_extracted` or `not_accessible` into complete review coverage. The public formatting may combine questions into coherent sections; a traceable map must show where every answer or justified non-answer is rendered.

Validate exactly one answer for each Q01–Q15, not merely an array of fifteen entries. The production schema must support binary and continuous outcomes, timepoints and multiple arms, plus result/claim/source relationships. A draft-only pilot schema with approval fixed to pending is not a production release schema; add exact-version approval and entitlement-safe public/premium serialization deliberately before deployment.

Examples to verify against current primary methodological guidance are RoB 2 for randomized results, an appropriate nonrandomized-comparative method, JBI case-series questions for uncontrolled series, and SYRCLE for animal experiments. Cell/ex-vivo reviews require an explicit protocol addressing controls, biological versus technical replication, identity, blinding where applicable and exposure plausibility. Do not claim formal appraisal without its required domains, evidence-backed judgments and qualified review. Avoid invented proprietary 0–100 quality scores. Reporting completeness, risk of bias, directness, precision and body-of-evidence certainty are distinct.

Keep regulatory assessment as a separate dated, jurisdiction/product/use-specific record. A paper's historical statement is not current regulatory verification. International reviews preserve original-language provenance and require bilingual scientific verification of consequential translated details; do not claim untranslated exclusives without evidence.

At an inline citation, expose the full citation plus a brief preview, an internal **Read Review** link for a completed review or a clearly labeled **Review pending** route, and an original-source link. A signed-out Read Review action opens that page's preview/login state and preserves the exact destination through login and intake. Support hover, keyboard and touch; preserve focus, Escape dismissal, citation-to-bibliography behavior, `ref-N` anchors and return position. No dead or misleading review links.
