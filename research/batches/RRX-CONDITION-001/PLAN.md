## What a condition page must answer

A condition is the starting point: which repurposed drugs have been investigated, which outcomes they may help, how convincing the evidence is, and which results are null, harmful, mixed or inconclusive. It is not a drug guide with its title changed, nor a reverse index of whichever drugs already have pages. A positive result is not an inclusion criterion. Preclinical rationale and registered studies without results remain distinguishable from demonstrated human benefit.

The initial 30-drug portfolio is a starting set, not a ceiling on options for a condition. Preserve those target identities and all current additions. Discover additional drugs when condition-specific evidence identifies them, screen their identity and repurposing eligibility, then add justified candidates through canonical integration. Do not generate unsupported pages simply to create links. The sparse drug–condition matrix is neither a fixed list of eligible pairs nor a requirement to populate every possible pair.

The pinned One Match Rule already states this breadth in Appendix C, and historical `fetch-30x30/requests.json` uses “Discover all relevant repurposed interventions” in its condition briefs. This addendum makes that intention an explicit prospective search, coverage and candidate-disposition contract. It does not rewrite those historical requests or claim their searches were completed.

## Condition scope and eligibility

For each canonical condition, author a filled brief before acquisition: definition and synonyms; inclusion/exclusion boundaries; relevant subtypes, disease stage and population; prevention versus treatment; symptomatic versus disease-modifying intent; background therapy, add-on and combination context; meaningful patient outcomes; and safety outcomes. Symptoms, biomarkers and surrogate endpoints do not become extra conditions. Related diagnoses cannot silently fill a condition's evidence rows. Preserve distinct population, subtype and timepoint strata when applicability differs.

Screen each candidate for resolved generic identity, original/established use and the proposed condition-specific use. Record source-backed repurposing eligibility separately from dated jurisdiction-specific regulatory status. A familiar drug name, off-label label, computational prediction or acquisition tag is insufficient. Established treatments can supply clearly labeled comparison/context but do not automatically become repurposing candidates. Unresolved identities or original-use boundaries remain pending with a next action. A combination has its own exact intervention context; evidence for one component does not establish the combination's effect.

## Discovery and source acquisition

Use a neutral, reproducible protocol with no favorable-result filter and no fixed paper or drug quota:

1. Reconcile existing condition-linked sources, exact study/report identities, scoped syntheses, current drug registry and historical search/screening logs. Reuse accepted work; distinguish an unsearched question from missing full text or unfinished extraction.
2. Search the condition and its verified synonyms with treatment/intervention/trial terms **without requiring a known drug name or the words repurposing/repositioning**. Add a separate repurposing-term search. This prevents seed lists and terminology from defining the candidate universe.
3. Search trial registries by condition and intervention class, including completed, discontinued and results-posted studies. Use relevant reviews as discovery/context and inspect their source-located references; resolve original reports separately. Chase backward and forward citations from relevant reports and reviews.
4. Search every identified drug candidate against the condition, relevant outcomes, safety, harms, null findings and failed/discontinued trials. Use exact verified names and identifiers, logging translations for each search surface.
5. Deduplicate against canonical reports and study families, screen relevance/eligibility, request only genuinely missing reports, versions, companions and review inputs, and preserve access/language limitations. A candidate outside the initial drug list is not excluded for that reason.

Named search/source locations: PubMed and Europe PMC for discovery/identity; ClinicalTrials.gov and any condition-relevant registry explicitly named in the filled brief; PMC, verified DOI/publisher pages and authorized repositories for lawful full text; official jurisdiction-specific sources for regulatory/label statements. The filled brief must contain actual translated queries, execution cutoff, source URLs, filters and access methods. This plan contains no claim that those searches have run. Never limit discovery to free full texts; inaccessible eligible reports remain in coverage.

Record each exact query, query family, database/backend, execution timestamp, cutoff, filters, observed count, returned/screened records, zero results and failures. Log the candidate's discovery provenance and every included, excluded, duplicate, context-only or held disposition with reasons. Additional candidates remain visible in the private discovery ledger until screened; they do not disappear because they lack an existing drug page.

## Structured handoff and scientific reuse

The additive [condition coverage schema](condition-coverage.schema.json) records searches, candidates, scope, dispositions, stage counts and gaps. It deliberately does not duplicate study extraction, outcome estimates, numerical locators, appraisal methods or Q01–Q15 answers. Those remain in the canonical contracts named in `plan.json`. Candidate-source references must resolve to exact original report/registry/context URLs and, when available, canonical source/report IDs and version/locator evidence.

The fundamental synthesis remains drug + exact condition + population/model + formulation/route + comparator + outcome + timepoint. Link each assessed candidate to the existing exact synthesis/outcome identity and scope fingerprint, retaining effect direction, directness, certainty, limitations, safety and exact sources in that canonical record. Use separate syntheses when scopes differ. Preserve independent study-family counts; reports, registry entries, supplements and reviews do not each become an independent study.

The same accepted synthesis must project both to the condition's intervention roundup and to the drug's relevant-condition evidence. No copied grades or independently authored duplicate scientific rows. The [condition-page contract](../../../docs/CONDITION-PAGES.md) specifies Overview → Interventions → Safety → Evidence → Research Feed → FAQs → Update History → References, with the condition table's four columns **Intervention / Outcome and finding / Evidence certainty / Evidence**. The view groups by intervention and outcome, with subordinate study design and exact panel sources; aggregation cannot conceal contradictory outcomes or imply head-to-head superiority from separate trials. Every cited scholarly paper retains its existing canonical Q01–Q15 review obligation, including secondary research.

Candidate discovery status, effect direction, evidence certainty, processing stage, human review and publication state remain independent. Human clinical findings, preclinical-only hypotheses, registry-only candidates and context-only sources are distinguishable. Null, harmful, mixed and inconclusive findings survive the same eligibility rules as favorable findings. Source absence is not evidence of no effect. Safety inputs include reported adverse events, withdrawals, relevant interactions and population-specific limits; trial exclusions do not automatically become contraindications.

## Coverage and completion gates

Maintain one private coverage record per condition, with the eligible-candidate denominator and unresolved candidate dispositions. Report discovered/screened/eligible candidates, acquired/extracted/verified reports, appraised/human-reviewed/released syntheses, synthesized options and independently reconciled study families in their declared units. Unknown values remain unknown; never convert unperformed work into zero.

“Complete” means complete against the named dated search protocol and its declared restrictions. It does not mean every possible drug or every paper in existence. A condition cannot pass coverage because the current evidence table renders, the original drug seeds have rows, or one source per condition was processed. Before a scoped completion claim, reconcile all planned search families, all returned candidates, all eligible reports and unresolved inputs, then assess whether any gap prevents that claim. A real no-eligible-evidence finding requires a completed scoped search. Technical JSON validation alone cannot certify scientific relevance, evidence completeness or clinical benefit.

Before new acquisition/worker handoff: fill the condition briefs and translated queries; reconcile live identities and source access; prepare actual worker-readable inputs, return layout/manifest and Q01–Q15 dependencies; validate schemas and cross-record relationships; independently check the scope; record a dated exact-hash GP decision.
