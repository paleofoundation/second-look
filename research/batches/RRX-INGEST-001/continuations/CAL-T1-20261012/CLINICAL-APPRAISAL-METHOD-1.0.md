# Qualitative clinical review method — version 1.0

Status: adopted as the immutable prospective method for separately approved first-tranche clinical source reviews on October 12, 2026. Each report still requires its own matching GP Plan PASS before extraction; this method supplies neither that PASS nor scientific acceptance. It complements the frozen multi-asset Q01–Q15/67-field review contract. It does not replace CAL07's preclinical method, confer a formal RoB2/ROBINS-I/GRADE assessment, create a numeric quality score or authorize publication.

Use the preserved design labels only to plan the review. Confirm the actual design from the assigned report, protocol, statistical analysis plan and companions after a fresh GP. One report and all its required assets produce one canonical review. Reports from the same cohort or trial retain separate version/report identities and explicit overlap; a second acquisition, analysis or treatment arm is not another independent study. Preserve existing result IDs and map any necessary refinements explicitly.

For every design, identify population, setting, eligibility, recruitment, exposure/formulation/route, comparator, outcome definition, timepoint and analysis unit before interpreting an estimate. Account for all result-bearing tables, figures and supplemental units, including null, contrary and harms findings. Separate prespecified, secondary, exploratory and post hoc analyses. Distinguish observed results, authors' claims and reviewer conclusions. Complete all 67 inputs and 15 substantive answers with actual locators and bounded appraisal; an answered paragraph never closes an unchecked input.

## Randomized or assigned-intervention trials

Appraise the actual sequence generation and allocation concealment separately from the word “randomized.” Describe participant, clinician, outcome-assessor and analyst masking where reported; assess implications for each relevant endpoint. Check baseline differences without treating a baseline significance test as proof of valid allocation.

Trace screened, randomized, treated, followed and analyzed populations by arm. Preserve each analysis population and estimand, including treatment assignment versus adherence-based analyses. Consider deviations, co-interventions, adherence, crossovers, exclusions, missing measurements and reasons. Do not treat complete-case or per-protocol findings as automatically equivalent to intention-to-treat effects.

Compare the dated protocol/SAP versions and reported changes with actual outcomes, timepoints and analyses. Preserve prospective versus retrospective changes, multiplicity, interim analyses, stopping/recruitment shortfalls and precision limitations. Failure to show a difference is not equivalence or proof of no effect.

For crossover, cluster or repeated-measures designs if present, review sequence/period, washout, possible carryover, clustering, paired analysis and the proper experimental unit. Repeated observations do not add independent participants. Do not assume a parallel-trial analysis applies.

Assess clinical versus surrogate relevance and instrument validity within the assigned scope. Preserve exact arm-specific denominators, scales, absolute and relative contrasts and uncertainty as reported. A physiologic change alone does not establish durable cognition, functioning, disease prevention or a patient benefit.

## Observational studies

State the source population, eligibility, exposure assignment, comparator, outcome ascertainment and time zero. Evaluate whether exposure measurement and follow-up align, including prevalent-user selection, immortal-time or other time-related bias, reverse causation, differential testing and detection, informative censoring and competing events where applicable.

Separate measured adjustment from residual or unmeasured confounding. Record prespecified covariates, model specification, missing covariate handling, weighting/matching methods, balance, overlap/positivity, exclusions and sensitivity analyses actually reported. An adjusted association or propensity score does not itself establish a causal effect. Check whether a comparison mixes different indications, disease severity, care access, treatment history or concomitant therapies.

Evaluate outcome validation, coding/measurement limitations, selection into the database and into the analysis, follow-up duration and generalizability. Preserve population restrictions and exposure timing. Avoid recasting an observational association as a randomized treatment benefit or as a recommendation to start therapy.

Compare narrative, abstract, tables and supplementary estimates without choosing whichever is most favorable. Retain conflicting denominators, directions or contrasts as explicit conflicts until their actual resolution is supported. Multiple models of overlapping participants do not create independent replication.

## Harms, integrity and conclusions

Describe how harms were sought, exposed denominators, observation time, event severity and withdrawals. Unreported harms or no statistically significant difference do not establish comprehensive safety. Trial exclusion criteria are not automatically contraindications.

Record authorship, funding, supplied materials and disclosed conflicts without using funding alone as a validity score. Check assigned version, corrections/retractions, registration/protocol links and companion completeness within a dated, explicitly permitted source scope. Missing registry or publisher access can require an acquisition gap; it cannot be replaced by a claim of no notice. Distinguish genuine unreported study details after inspection from unfinished source work.

Conclude in outcome-specific prose: what the exact evidence supports, what threatens that interpretation, which populations and comparisons it applies to, and what evidence could change the conclusion. Avoid collapsing several endpoints into a drug-wide certainty label. Use the project's separate evidence-grading procedure only in a separately authorized synthesis operation.

Use exact reported numerical scalars and preserve qualified quantities without false precision. Graph estimates, recalculation, conversion or derived estimates remain outside the frozen full-review numerical contract. Every consequential numeric table/figure needs visual inspection and an independent calibration check. The independent reviewer verifies the full source packet, result/claim links, all inputs, all answers, appraisal and limitations; audit and acceptance bind the exact candidate hash. Human approval, release, public rendering and canonical import remain separate gates.
