# CAL-T1 private scientific review protocol, version 1.0

This is a source-based qualitative review protocol, not formal GRADE, a formal
SYRCLE/RoB2 assessment, a numeric quality score or human clinical approval.
The canonical One Match Rule Q01–Q15 map and its 67 required inputs govern the
review. A report's scientific limitations do not disappear when its review is
complete. Complete means the declared source investigation and appraisal are
finished and independently checked, with remaining study limitations explicit.

For CAL-07 inspect the entire NIH author-manuscript HTML, all seven original
figure images, all ten physical pages of the matching publisher supplement, and
the two received integrity metadata responses. Distinguish that source version
from the inaccessible final publisher text. Preserve the main byline and the
supplement/final-index byline discrepancy; do not invent an authorship correction.
Document the dated correction/version check scope and any inaccessible surface.
No assertion of an exhaustive worldwide correction or replication search follows
from these checks. Related-paper discovery remains outside this bounded review.

Account for each experiment and result-bearing section/panel, including null,
contradictory and mechanistic control results. Separate animals, tumors, cell
cultures, technical replicates and repeated measurements. Preserve exact model,
allocation/comparator, formulation, route/exposure, endpoint and timepoint.
Retain earlier result/claim identifiers when the underlying finding is unchanged;
add explicit mappings when refinement changes its scope. Main and companion
assets have distinct IDs/hashes but one canonical report/review identity.

Appraise the preclinical work in explicit prose across these domains:

1. Control choice, allocation/randomization and baseline comparability.
2. Blinding and outcome ascertainment, including subjective tumor measurements.
3. Biological versus technical replication, experiment independence, clustering
   and the unit used by each analysis.
4. Missing observations, exclusions, attrition and reported reasons.
5. Outcome prespecification, selective reporting, multiple comparisons and
   distinction between primary, exploratory and mechanistic endpoints.
6. Estimate precision, reported uncertainty, analysis suitability and any
   internal inconsistency. A curve or error bar is not an exact numeric table.
7. Exposure/formulation plausibility and limits translating cells or animals to
   human disease; concentration comparisons do not establish clinical efficacy.
8. Harms ascertainment and detection limits; no reported toxicity is not evidence
   of comprehensive safety.
9. Disclosures, funding, supplied materials and version/integrity limitations.

Use exact quoted evidence and native/PDF/image locators for reported facts.
Distinguish authors' interpretations from the reviewer's appraisal. Do not infer
randomization, absent harms or direct target binding from suggestive results.
Missing design information may be not_reported only after complete inspection of
all relevant assigned assets. Inapplicable fields require design-specific reasons.
Unread or inaccessible required source inputs keep completion pending.

Quantitative claims preserve their units and scope. Exact scalar numbers must
match the source; approximate/qualified values retain the qualifier and do not
become falsely precise JSON scalars. Graph-only values are described qualitatively
unless a separately adopted estimation protocol justifies numeric extraction.
Derived estimates require a separately adopted calculation contract. Visually
inspect each consequential table/figure and audit all calibration numbers.

The independent verifier reads the actual assigned sources and audits all Q
answers, all input dispositions, result/claim relationships, quantitative support,
appraisal and limitations. The verifier must be distinct from the author and may
return corrections or HOLD; it does not confer human approval or release. The
integrator alone may accept and import the exact private candidate. Any revision
invalidates its old acceptance. Preserve the original private checkpoints.

Later first-tranche clinical reports need their own prospective design-appropriate
appraisal brief before extraction; this preclinical protocol cannot silently
stand in for trial or observational-study appraisal.
