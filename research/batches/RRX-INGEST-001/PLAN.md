## Records and processing

Preserve separate source-asset, paper/report, study-family, experiment, arm, outcome, result, claim, synthesis and review identities. Normalize verified identifiers, retain conflicting identifiers for adjudication, and never merge fuzzy titles or different DOI reports automatically. One study family may have several reports. Original-study counts exclude context, protocols and companion files.

The fundamental synthesis scope includes drug, exact indication, population/model, formulation/route, comparator, outcome and timepoint. Preserve existing IDs with explicit exact-scope mappings; ambiguous migrations stop instead of overwriting another endpoint. A report can support several page destinations without becoming several studies. Acquisition request tags alone never justify page fan-out.

Source extraction preserves exact bytes/hash and PDF physical/printed page, table/figure, or stable native HTML/XML/EPUB locator. Record reported estimates, units, numerator/denominator, uncertainty, arms, randomized/analyzed counts and source quotations independently from interpretation. Consequential table/figure numbers require visual checks. Unsupported formats are held until a format-specific locator contract is adopted and calibrated.

Mechanism records distinguish original-use and condition-specific rationale, target/pathway/action, tested/proposed/inferred causal steps, model/tissue, exposure/duration, contrary evidence and translation limits. Clinical outcomes do not substitute for mechanistic experiments. PRIMARY and CONTEXT remain separate.

Every cited scholarly paper has one canonical review obligation, including secondary research. Carry the pinned Q01–Q15 input map into every worker packet. Preserve exactly one answer per question, source locators, plural study/experiment links and question-level gaps. A substantive answer may explain genuinely unreported information after source inspection; unchecked, inaccessible or unextracted required inputs remain unfinished. Candidate, scientific, human and release states remain separate. Source or answer changes invalidate dependent assessments and exact-version approvals.
