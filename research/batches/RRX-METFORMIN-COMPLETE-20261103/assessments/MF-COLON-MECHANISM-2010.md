# Colorectal crypt-foci pilot: within-group results and an unvalidated clinical surrogate

Private source assessment. Independent verification and human approval are pending.


## What question was actually tested?

Test low-dose metformin effects on rectal aberrant crypt foci. One-month pilot, registry UMIN000001241 identified. Crypt-foci counts and epithelial proliferation. Not colorectal cancer incidence.


## Who or what was studied?

Nondiabetic adults with endoscopically identified crypt foci. Yokohama City University; major hepatic/renal disease, pregnancy, CRC and NSAIDs excluded. 1342 screened, 629 with foci, 26 randomized: 12 metformin/14 untreated; 9/14 analyzed. Very selected and predominantly male sample.


## What exactly was used?

Metformin, formulation/product not specified in the inspected pages. Oral 250 mg/day for one month. Prior screening/polypectomy when needed. One month. Not a recommended chemoprevention schedule.


## What was the comparator?

No treatment, not placebo. Participants assigned to two groups. Randomization stated; sequence/concealment not described. Endoscopic and laboratory assessments masked; participants not placebo-blinded. Baseline and one-month comparison. Lack of placebo and allocation detail increase uncertainty.


## What was measured and does it matter?

Crypt-foci count; PCNA proliferation and TUNEL apoptosis. Magnifying colonoscopy with methylene blue; two masked observers; biopsies. Baseline to one month. Surrogate lesion and cellular markers. No established cancer-risk reduction threshold. No cancer endpoint.


## How large was the result?

Metformin foci 8.78±6.45 to 5.11±4.99; control 7.23±6.65 to 7.56±6.75. Nine metformin and fourteen control analyzed. Mean±SD foci per participant. Metformin within-arm P=.007; control within-arm P=.609. No reported between-group change estimate with CI. Significant in one arm and nonsignificant in another does not establish a significant treatment interaction.


## What happened to the other observations?

Three of twelve metformin participants lacked endpoint: one stopped early, two refused colonoscopy. All missing endpoints in the metformin arm. Pill adherence not quantified. Complete-case exclusion of three metformin participants. 23 evaluable participants. Participant, not crypt count or microscopic field. Biopsy fields are technical observations.


## What harms were sought and found?

Brief safety narrative without detailed surveillance methods. Twelve assigned, nine evaluable metformin participants; safety denominator not explicit. No side effects reported, including diarrhea, hypoglycemia or lactic acidosis. One early treatment stop; cause not stated. One month. Cannot infer long-term or rare-event safety.


## How could the design or analysis mislead?

Tiny sample, one-arm missingness, no placebo and within-arm testing. Randomization not described sufficiently to exclude selection bias. Registry history and supplementary Figure S1 not recovered. Several foci categories and laboratory tests. No between-group CI; substantial uncertainty. Control age SD printed 90.7 years, evidently discordant with the scale; not silently repaired. No causal effect size calculated from these data.


## Does this apply to the reader or product?

Selected nondiabetic adults with crypt foci. 250 mg daily for one month. Preliminary surrogate signal only. Does not establish prevention of adenomas or cancer, or effect in average-risk adults. Later adenoma trial is a different study and endpoint.


## Is this independent evidence?

Hosono 2010 human pilot, UMIN000001241. Related animal studies and Higurashi 2016 adenoma trial. Distinct from animal experiments in the same research program. Current inventory reconciliation. Do not mistake the adjacent leptin paper in the archive for this study.


## Who conducted and supported it?

Kunihiro Hosono, Hiroki Endo, Hirokazu Takahashi and colleagues; Atsushi Nakajima corresponding. Yokohama City University and National Cancer Center Japan. Japanese ministry, biomedical innovation, Yokohama foundation and JST grants. Metformin supplier not stated in inspected pages. No potential conflicts disclosed. Funding/COI located on p1082, not the unrelated preceding article.

Final reference page1083 and supplementary Figure S1 absent. UMIN000001241 stated, separate record not audited. No correction/retraction identified in retrieved records. November 4, 2026. Readable images used because embedded text extraction was empty.


## Which claims does it support or contradict?

Early human mechanistic pilot. Indirect rationale, not clinical prevention proof. Authors infer promise; guide emphasizes within-group analysis and surrogate endpoint. Never cite as a positive cancer-prevention trial.


## What should this change in our understanding?

Small preliminary foci changes motivated further research. Controlled change magnitude and long-term cancer relevance remain unknown. Larger randomized between-group clinical-outcome evidence required. The 2016 lesion trial adds evidence but still does not prove reduced cancer mortality.


## Overall appraisal

A small unblinded pilot with blinded assessment, substantial metformin-arm attrition and within-group hypothesis tests. It supplies preliminary biological context, not a reliable between-group cancer-prevention effect.
