# Schizophrenia parent trial: preliminary cognitive benefit, not established functional recovery

Private source assessment. Independent verification and human approval are pending.


## What question was actually tested?

Test adjunctive metformin for cognitive impairment in schizophrenia. Current registry lists MCCB at 12 weeks; paper follows participants for 24 weeks. Cognitive test performance and its relationship to metabolic and brain-connectivity changes. The longer follow-up is not silently treated as the registered primary timepoint.


## Who or what was studied?

Clinically stable adults aged 18–65, predominantly young, with schizophrenia. Second Xiangya Hospital, China; illness at most 5 years, at least 10% antipsychotic-associated weight gain; diabetes and major physical illness excluded. 72 randomized, 48 metformin and 24 control; 59 completed 24 weeks, 39 and 20 respectively. The completion population differs from the randomized population.


## What exactly was used?

Lilinghengtai metformin tablets. Oral titration from 500 mg evening to 1500 mg/day in three doses by day 5, or maximum tolerated dose. Fixed baseline antipsychotic treatment in both groups. Assessments baseline, 12 and 24 weeks. This is adjunctive treatment, not replacement of antipsychotics.


## What was the comparator?

Antipsychotics alone, without placebo. 2:1 assignment. Computer-generated random numbers. Open-label treatment; independent cognitive evaluator blinded. September 2017–April 2021. Participant and clinician expectations may affect performance despite evaluator blinding.


## What was measured and does it matter?

Cognition primary; metabolic, symptom and imaging analyses additional. Chinese MCCB composite and seven domains; PANSS, depression scale and resting-state fMRI. 12 and 24 weeks. Standardized cognitive test, not direct daily-function or quality-of-life improvement. Post hoc responder threshold at least 5 MCCB points; functional clinical importance was not independently established. A score gain is not proof of better everyday function.


## How large was the result?

Adjusted 24-week composite advantage 3.43 points for metformin. Post hoc responders 28/39 versus 6/20 completers. Composite 95% CI 1.83–5.02 points; responder OR 5.94, CI 1.82–19.40. Between-arm cognitive advantage; domain differences also reported, including processing speed 3.75 and verbal learning 5.71 points. Responder interval is wide; no equivalently verified functional outcome estimate. Do not substitute within-arm metabolic improvement for the between-arm cognitive effect.


## What happened to the other observations?

13 of 72 did not complete 24 weeks. Linear mixed models accommodate missing follow-up under assumptions; eight lacked week-24 MRI because of COVID restrictions. Pill returns; reported mean adherence 96.1%, all over 80%. Low baseline cognitive-deficit participants excluded from imaging/correlation analyses; endpoint-specific samples differ. Cognition mixed models; Table 1 metabolic analysis labels 40 and 23, despite 48 and 24 randomized. Participant is the experimental unit; brain voxels and visits are not independent patients. The denominators require endpoint-specific reporting rather than a single trial-wide number.


## What harms were sought and found?

Treatment-Emergent Symptom Scale at each visit. Safety report explicitly uses 45 metformin recipients for decreased appetite. Decreased appetite 15/45, 33.3%, versus 0% in control; other differences described as nonsignificant. Complete withdrawal reasons and adverse-event Table S9 are not in the acquired main report. 24 weeks. Neither a nonsignificant comparison nor the authors' general safety wording excludes uncommon harms.


## How could the design or analysis mislead?

Open-label small single-center study with attrition and selected imaging subsets. Randomization helps baseline balance; expectations and post-randomization selection remain. Registry 12-week primary versus emphasized 24-week report; full historical protocol comparison unavailable. Numerous domains, correlations and mediation tests; imaging used cluster FWE correction but this does not correct every analysis. Cognitive CI excludes zero, while small responder and imaging subsets remain imprecise. Published denominators differ by cognitive, metabolic and safety analyses; do not harmonize by assumption. Correlational mediation cannot establish a causal biological pathway.


## Does this apply to the reader or product?

Stable early-course schizophrenia with antipsychotic-associated weight gain. Adjunctive oral 1500 mg/day, not all psychiatric populations or doses. Cognition test performance, not symptom remission or independent living. Does not establish benefit in diabetes, older adults, unstable psychosis or long-duration disease. The broad term cognitive enhancer would overstate this evidence.


## Is this independent evidence?

One parent randomized cohort. CP017 is the 2025 exploratory TCA/hippocampal analysis of this same cohort. 58 metabolite and 33 imaging participants in CP017 overlap this trial. Current guide references reconciled; no exhaustive replication review. Count one trial, not a parent and a new mechanistic RCT.


## Who conducted and supported it?

Tiannan Shao, Jing Huang, Renrong Wu and colleagues. Second Xiangya Hospital, Central South University. National Natural Science Foundation of China 81622018 and 82072096. Brand/manufacturer specified; donation not established. Authors declare no competing interests. Funding and disclosed interests do not replace design appraisal.


## Is the source current and intact?

Published Translational Psychiatry 2023 report, DOI 10.1038/s41398-023-02616-x. Full text acquired and assessed. Separate full protocol and supplemental safety/flow tables not successfully acquired. NCT03271866 current record inspected; first posted September 5, 2017, after listed August 28 start. No correction or retraction identified in retrieved article/metadata. November 3–4, 2026. This remains a main-report assessment with explicit supplementary gaps.


## Which claims does it support or contradict?

Schizophrenia cognitive benefit and mechanism. Supports a preliminary cognitive test signal, with important bias and functional-outcome limits. Authors' stronger efficacy language is narrowed editorially; brain changes are associations. Use parent results for clinical estimates and CP017 only for exploratory context.


## What should this change in our understanding?

Promising but unconfirmed adjunctive cognitive effect in a selected population. Clinical importance, durability, real-life function and mechanism remain uncertain. Larger placebo-controlled trials with prespecified cognition and functioning outcomes could change this judgment. No independent clinical replication is supplied by CP017.


## Overall appraisal

A positive cognitive test finding with substantial limits: open-label treatment, small sample, multiple analyses and incomplete functional assessment. The later mechanistic paper is a companion, not independent replication.
