# Myeloma cell and xenograft experiments: preclinical evidence only

Private source assessment. Independent verification and human approval are pending.


## What question was actually tested?

Test growth inhibition and AMPK/mTOR pathways. No preregistered experimental analysis plan reported. Cell proliferation, cell cycle and xenograft growth. No patient survival question was tested.


## Who or what was studied?

Human myeloma cell lines RPMI8226/U266 and NOD/SCID mice. Cell-bank lines; five-week-old immunodeficient mice with subcutaneous xenografts. Twelve mice randomized, six per group; cell experiments in triplicate with repeats. Human-derived cells are not human participants.


## What exactly was used?

Sigma metformin dissolved in PBS. Cells 5–80 mM, 24–72 hours; mice oral 250 mg/kg/day for 21 days. AMPK siRNA, Compound C and 3-MA in mechanistic experiments. Serial tumor volume/body weight every three days, terminal tissue at day 21. No direct translation of these doses to a patient.


## What was the comparator?

Untreated/PBS controls; scrambled siRNA controls. Two xenograft cohorts; assay-specific cell comparisons. Mice randomly assigned; sequence unspecified. Blinded tumor measurement or analysis not reported. Treatment after tumors reached approximately 0.5 by 0.5 cm. Model is established xenograft growth, not naturally occurring myeloma.


## What was measured and does it matter?

Viability, proliferation, cycle arrest, autophagy, pathway phosphorylation and tumor size. CCK8, EdU, flow cytometry, electron microscopy, western blot and calipers. Hours in cells, three weeks in mice. Laboratory surrogates, no clinical response or survival. No patient-important change threshold. Subcutaneous tumor volume is not a validated surrogate for patient survival here.


## How large was the result?

48-hour IC50 20.2±1.2 mM RPMI8226 and 17.9±1.1 mM U266. Cell triplicates; xenograft six mice per group. Mean±SD; lower tumor growth reported in Figure 6. G0/G1 arrest and reduced proliferation, not increased apoptosis. Exact tumor-volume contrast/CI not tabulated; no digitized estimate claimed. Qualitative tumor direction is used, not invented bar-chart precision.


## What happened to the other observations?

Animal flow/attrition not explicitly tabulated. Missing values and exclusion rules not fully described. Daily gavage stated; no measured plasma concentration. Cell-line and assay selection limits scope. Assay-specific cultures and twelve mice. Independent culture or animal, not image field. Triplicates and repeated fields do not create patient counts.


## What harms were sought and found?

Body weight monitored as a limited toxicity indicator. Six metformin-treated mice. No significant body-weight difference reported. Toxicity-related withdrawals not specified. Twenty-one days. Stable weight is not comprehensive toxicology or proof of human safety.


## How could the design or analysis mislead?

Limited animal n, unclear masking, cell-line dependence and high concentrations. Knockdown plus drug inhibitors provide triangulation but have specificity limitations. No experimental preregistration identified. Many t-tests across doses/timepoints/pathways without a stated global adjustment. No clinical effect estimate or uncertainty. Figure 5 legend duplicates P<.05 for one/two stars; model Figure 6 says triplicate despite six mice per group. Do not overinterpret significance symbols or replicate descriptions.


## Does this apply to the reader or product?

Preclinical myeloma models. Millimolar culture exposure and mouse gavage do not establish achievable human tumor exposure. Rationale for research, not treatment benefit. No evidence here of reduced myeloma mortality or safe combination therapy. Authors’ “approved and safe” language refers to another indication, not this experimental use.


## Is this independent evidence?

2018 myeloma laboratory study. External observational reports are background citations, not its own patient data. Multiple assay panels belong to one experimental program. Current guide inventory only. Do not count background reports without assessment.


## Who conducted and supported it?

Yan Wang, Wenbin Xu, Zixun Yan, Weili Zhao, Jianqing Mi, Junmin Li and Hua Yan. Shanghai Jiao Tong University-related animal/laboratory setting. National Natural Science Foundation of China grants 81302038 and 81600155. Commercial Sigma drug and named assay suppliers. Authors declared no competing interests. No inference about validity from funding alone.


## Is the source current and intact?

Published 2018, Journal of Experimental & Clinical Cancer Research. Full XML acquired and inspected. No separate supplementary result used. Animal ethics reported; no human trial registration. No correction/retraction identified in retrieved records. November 3–4, 2026. Not a forensic assessment of blot images.


## Which claims does it support or contradict?

Antimyeloma mechanisms. Indirect laboratory support only. Growth inhibition observed; clinical promise is authors’ extrapolation. No clinical efficacy claim.


## What should this change in our understanding?

Laboratory activity is insufficient to recommend metformin for myeloma. Human exposure, efficacy and combination safety unknown. Direct controlled human trials with patient outcomes required. Keep the preclinical label prominent.


## Overall appraisal

Cell and xenograft activity supplies a hypothesis, not clinical efficacy. High cell concentrations, small unblinded animal comparisons and limited toxicology prevent therapeutic extrapolation.
