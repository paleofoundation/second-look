# Schizophrenia TCA companion: exploratory associations in the existing trial

Private source assessment. Independent verification and human approval are pending.


## What question was actually tested?

Explore TCA metabolites, hippocampal connectivity and cognitive change after adjunctive metformin. Explicitly post hoc exploratory analysis. A proposed biological explanation of an existing trial signal, not a fresh efficacy test. Preserve exploratory status.


## Who or what was studied?

Human schizophrenia participants from the parent cohort, median age 22. Stable illness and antipsychotic treatment; selected available plasma and follow-up. Parent 72; metabolite analysis 58, 40 metformin and 18 control; final MRI 33, 21 and 12. Biomarker and imaging subsets are smaller than the randomized trial.


## What exactly was used?

Metformin tablets as in the parent report. Up to 500 mg three times daily orally, or maximum tolerated dose. Stable antipsychotics, doses converted to chlorpromazine equivalents. Baseline, 12 and 24 weeks. Use the parent for the product and titration history.


## What was the comparator?

Antipsychotics alone. Inherited 2:1 randomized parent allocation. Computer random-number generator in parent. Open-label treatment; trained evaluators, parent cognitive evaluator masked. Samples collected in the September 2017–April 2021 parent trial. Post hoc sample availability is not randomized.


## What was measured and does it matter?

Post hoc metabolites and imaging; cognition correlations exploratory. LC-MS/MS for nine plasma metabolites, MCCB and 3-T resting-state fMRI. Weeks 12 and 24. Circulating biomarkers and functional connectivity, not validated surrogates for recovery. No validated clinical benefit threshold for these metabolite/connectivity changes. Plasma concentrations do not directly measure neuronal TCA flux.


## How large was the result?

At week 24 adjusted lactate means 194.34 versus 342.43 micrograms/mL; pyruvate 43.45 versus 81.62. Metabolite analysis 40 versus 18; MRI 21 versus 12. Lactate between-arm difference −148.09 micrograms/mL, CI −184.61 to −111.57; pyruvate −38.17, CI −47.18 to −29.17. Abstract lactate −80.81 and pyruvate −17.51 are within-metformin changes, not between-arm effects. Imaging contrasts −0.334 at week 12 and 0.284 at week 24; exploratory correlations lack multiplicity-adjusted confirmation. This resolves the crucial within-arm versus between-arm distinction.


## What happened to the other observations?

14 parent participants absent from plasma analysis; MRI selected 41 then excluded eight after preprocessing. Last observation carried forward for missing TCA/cognition; sensitivity analysis on observed and imputed data; MRI not imputed. Parent reports high pill adherence; no independent biomarker adherence confirmation. Availability and image-quality exclusions; motion threshold 3 mm or 3 degrees in supplement. Post hoc available-case subset of randomized cohort. Participant, not metabolite, brain region, voxel or repeated sample. Selection and imputation can distort association estimates.


## What harms were sought and found?

No new systematic harms analysis in this mechanistic report. Biomarker subset is not a newly exposed safety cohort. Parent reports decreased appetite; companion cannot independently confirm safety. No new treatment-withdrawal analysis in CP017. Same 24-week exposure window. Absence of safety results here is not absence of adverse events.


## How could the design or analysis mislead?

Post hoc selection, small imaging sample, open-label parent and multiple correlational analyses. Metabolic status, antipsychotic exposure and other factors may confound mediator-outcome associations. Analysis not prospectively specified; current registry cognition primary does not prospectively validate these exploratory tests. Bonferroni used for some metabolite contrasts and FWE for imaging; correlations/interactions deliberately use raw P values. Large metabolite contrasts do not resolve precision of clinical benefit; MRI n=33. Abstract emphasizes within-arm changes; Table 3 is the between-arm comparison. Do not infer TCA activation causes cognitive improvement.


## Does this apply to the reader or product?

Young stable schizophrenia cohort with weight gain; not general cognitive decline. Same adjunctive metformin regimen as parent. Mechanistic hypothesis, not a treatment-response biomarker ready for clinical use. Peripheral metabolites and selected imaging do not establish central metabolic flux or mediation. This cannot support an independent Parkinson or dementia claim.


## Is this independent evidence?

Same trial family as MF-SCHIZOPHRENIA-PARENT-2023. Follow-up metabolomic/imaging report. All analyzed participants are drawn from the parent trial. Bounded identity reconciliation, no comprehensive replication search. Adds mechanistic observations, zero new randomized trials.


## Who conducted and supported it?

Jingda Cai, Jingmei Xiao, Tiannan Shao, Hualin Cai, Renrong Wu and colleagues. Second Xiangya Hospital and collaborating institutions. Chinese national science grants, Hunan innovation support and institutional clinical specialty research support. Assay standards purchased from specified suppliers; no inference of sponsor efficacy control. Authors declare no competing interests. The assay validation reports linearity, precision and recovery, not proof of clinical surrogacy.


## Is the source current and intact?

Published BMC Medicine 2025, DOI 10.1186/s12916-025-04218-4. Full report and supplemental DOCX acquired; assay, eligibility, missing-data and sensitivity tables inspected. Additional file 1 includes LC-MS/MS calibration and imaging preprocessing. NCT03271866 current registry and parent compared; full historical registry audit not performed. No correction/retraction identified in retrieved current source records. November 3–4, 2026. Independent reproduction of the raw-data analysis remains pending.


## Which claims does it support or contradict?

Potential TCA/connectivity explanation of cognitive signal. Indirect mechanistic support only; not independent efficacy evidence. Observations are associations; author interpretation of activation is a hypothesis. Use no clinical-benefit grade from these biomarkers alone.


## What should this change in our understanding?

Exploratory biological changes merit further study but do not prove a treatment mechanism. Causality, reproducibility and relation to everyday function remain unknown. Preregistered replicated mediator analyses with clinical outcomes and direct metabolic measurements are needed. Keep this report in the same family as the parent.


## Overall appraisal

Substantive post hoc mechanistic assessment. The abstract within-arm changes must not be mislabeled treatment effects; Table 3 supplies the proper between-arm contrasts. Correlations do not establish mediation or add a second randomized trial.
