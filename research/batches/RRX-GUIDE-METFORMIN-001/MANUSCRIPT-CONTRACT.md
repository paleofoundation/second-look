# One source for the metformin consumer edition

This is an authoring contract and content outline, **not a substantive guide or approved sample**. Write the manuscript only after the topic's required evidence ingestion and assessment pass. Keep the declared evidence cutoff distinct from last editorial review and release dates. General readers are the primary audience; no physician edition is in this release.

| Section | Required evidence/reader value | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| Quick orientation | Established role versus each investigated use; main findings in ordinary language | Exact official context plus accepted outcome-specific syntheses; no drug-wide efficacy grade |
| How it works | Original action and investigated mechanisms, with plain visual explanations | Every step bound to a reviewed source/result; tested, proposed and inferred distinguished, model/exposure limits retained |
| Infection and Long COVID | Acute outcomes, prevention after acute illness and established-illness treatment | COVID-OUT corrected family, complete ACTIV-6, Korea and valid current REVIVE disposition; never mix prevention/treatment |
| Neurological conditions | Parkinson clinical trial versus animal rationale; schizophrenia parent versus exploratory companion | Shared schizophrenia family, source-bound outcomes and uncertainty; biomarkers not mediation or clinical proof |
| Cancers | Separate breast, lung, prostate, pancreatic and colorectal-prevention chapters; myeloma laboratory context | Clinical setting, comparator, co-treatment and endpoints retained; no cross-cancer efficacy transfer |
| Physical function | Healthy exercise participants versus older frail adults | MASTERS/MET-PREVENT full reviews; outdated narrative review cannot stand in for later trial |
| Understanding different results | Side-by-side population, timing, formulation, control, outcome, duration, missingness and uncertainty | Compared estimates actually comparable or explicitly distinct; explain conflicts without invented pooled effects |
| Safety and formulations | Supported effects, interactions, contraindications/precautions, monitoring questions and IR/ER context | Current exact official sources and relevant trial harms; critical safety remains free publicly |
| Preparing a medical discussion | Condition-specific questions, applicability checklist, printable medication/discussion worksheet, glossary | Educational wording; no universal schedule or personalized instruction; worksheet is user-controlled and not account intake |
| Sources and updates | Linked references, source/review versions, actual contributors and edition changes | No invented reviewer credits; clinical/legal/editorial approvals bind exact web/PDF/summary/sample/marketing hashes |

Paid material adds comparative synthesis, effect-size explanations when supported, conflicting-findings discussion, applicability checklists, expanded FAQs and printable tools. It is not an article export. Cite every factual sentence and figure/table datum. Preserve report IDs, family IDs, result/claim IDs, locators, review version, integrity state and human approval separately. No unapproved factual claim can enter an approved public sample merely because the sample is short.

Use one versioned manuscript to build protected web, full PDF, sample and concise printable summary. Each item requires a stable block ID, audience/asset inclusion flags and source mappings; a sample allowlist must contain only specifically approved sample blocks. Check identical claims/numbers across outputs. Web body and all files stay private until server authorization; only approved sample content is public. Retain immutable edition history and product-level purchase ownership according to the owner-approved commercial contract.

PDF acceptance includes selectable text, readable single-column prose, usable bookmarks and source links, no clipped tables or labels, and visual inspection of every page. Use the reusable existing PDF renderer with RemapRx brand tokens. The Examine document supplies only structural lessons—orientation, substantive entries, questions, references and credits. Its medical claims, wording, graphics, classifications and internal instructions are not adopted.

Before release: independent source/numerical audit, complete study-review coverage for cited scholarly reports, actual human editorial approval, qualified independent clinical and applicable legal review, five real representative-reader tests with the repository's comprehension criterion, exact web/PDF/sample/summary/marketing hashes and asset access tests. Each unperformed check stays pending; technical builds and AI-authored reviews cannot mark these checks complete.
